# InspectOS 90-Day Recruitment Execution Roadmap

## TL;DR

This roadmap translates the talent strategy from Prompts 1–5 into a concrete **90-day execution plan** with defined milestones, budget, and risk contingencies. **Phase 1 (Days 1–30)** builds the foundation: job descriptions, careers page, OE registration, legal infrastructure, and initial job postings across 5 free channels — **budget: €2,800**. **Phase 2 (Days 31–60)** activates full multi-channel sourcing, conducts first screening and vetting cycles, and begins trial inspections — **budget: €4,200**. **Phase 3 (Days 61–90)** converts vetted candidates to active inspectors, onboards the first intern cohort, and establishes quality baselines — **budget: €4,500**. **Total 90-day budget: €11,500**, realistic for a pre-seed proptech startup. The plan targets **5 vetted contractors** and **2 full-time senior inspectors** by Day 90, with **coverage of both Lisbon and Porto metropolitan areas**. Three primary risks could derail the timeline: (1) OE-registered candidate shortage, mitigated by Brazilian reciprocity channel; (2) delayed OEP admission for Brazilian candidates, mitigated by interim trainee structure; and (3) quality failures in trial inspections, mitigated by calibrated trial properties and conditional pass protocols.

## 5. Risk Register

| **Risk** | **Likelihood** | **Impact** | **Mitigation** | **Contingency** | **Owner** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **R1: Insufficient OE-registered candidates apply** | Medium | High | Lead with Brazilian CREA→OE channel; activate university pipeline early | Increase job board spend; engage recruitment agency (Michael Page) | Operations |
| **R2: Brazilian candidate OEP admission delayed >90 days** | Medium | Medium | Pre-qualify CREA status before application; interim trainee role keeps candidate engaged | Convert to Angolan-style support role; or release with option to return | Operations |
| **R3: Trial inspection failures exceed 50%** | Low | High | Calibrate trial properties; use conditional pass (65–79%) with retraining | Extend shadowing period; provide additional coaching before re-trial | Quality Manager |
| **R4: Senior candidate accepts counter-offer** | Medium | High | Move fast (offer within 48h of trial); competitive Total Reward Statement; equity discussion | Maintain pipeline of 3+ senior candidates simultaneously; don't rely on one finalist | Founder |
| **R5: Legal/contract delays** | Low | Medium | Engage lawyer in Week 1; parallel-track contract drafting with candidate vetting | Use standard Portuguese employment contract templates as interim measure | Legal counsel |
| **R6: IEFP internship window missed** | Low | Medium | Monitor IEFP portal for next opening; maintain relationship with university career offices | Offer private internship (higher cost: €960/month fully borne by InspectOS) | Operations |
| **R7: Quality inconsistency across new hires** | Medium | High | 100% audit for first month; standardized templates; peer review | Pull underperforming inspector from independent work; retrain or terminate | Quality Manager |

### 5.1 Go/No-Go Criteria by Phase Gate

| **Phase Gate** | **Go Criteria** | **No-Go Contingency** |
|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1→2 (Day 30)** | ≥20 applications received; OE employer registration confirmed; contracts ready; trial properties secured | Delay Phase 2 by 1 week; increase advertising spend; expedite legal review |
| **Phase 2→3 (Day 60)** | ≥3 candidates passed trial inspection; ≥1 senior finalist identified; ≥2 contractors ready to sign | Extend Phase 2 by 2 weeks; activate agency channel; expand Brazilian outreach |
| **Phase 3 Completion (Day 90)** | ≥5 total active inspectors (incl. existing); ≥1 senior FT offer accepted; quality baseline established | Extend timeline to Day 120; adjust targets; focus on contractor-only model until senior found |
