## SEARCH METHODOLOGY

**Search Waves Executed:**
1. General English home inspection report PDF searches
2. Regional variants (UK structural survey, Australian building inspection, etc.)
3. Government and public record sources
4. Professional association standards and templates
5. Commercial inspection company samples
6. Academic and professional guide references
7. Multi-lingual searches (French for Quebec)

**Search Operators Used:**
- `filetype:pdf` for direct PDF access
- `site:.gov` / `site:.gov.uk` / `site:.gov.au` / `site:.gov.hk` for government sources
- Regional TLD filters (`site:.co.uk`, `site:.com.au`, `site:.co.nz`, etc.)
- `"sample" OR "example"` for template discovery
- Association names (RICS, InterNACHI, AS, NZS) for standard-compliant reports
