# Position 1: Senior Property Inspector
### Lisbon / Porto | Full-Time or Contractor (Recibo Verde)

## About InspectOS

InspectOS is Portugal's leading proptech property inspection platform. We produce **court-admissible technical inspection reports** for residential property buyers, sellers, and investors, delivered by a network of OE-registered engineers and certified inspectors. Operating in Lisbon and Porto, we combine engineering rigour with digital efficiency — every report is produced using our proprietary platform, follows a 100+ checkpoint methodology, and meets the standards required in a post-Simplex Urbanístico (DL 10/2024) market.

## The Role

We are looking for an **experienced civil engineer with Ordem dos Engenheiros (OE) registration and signing authority** to join our team as a Senior Property Inspector. You will be the **quality anchor** of our inspection operation — conducting pre-purchase inspections, producing court-admissible reports, mentoring junior inspectors, and ensuring every deliverable meets InspectOS standards.

This role is available as **full-time employment (Contrato sem Termo)** or **contractor (Recibo Verde / Trabalhador Independente)**. We are flexible on structure for the right candidate.

## Key Responsibilities

**Inspection & Reporting (60%)**
- Conduct comprehensive pre-purchase property inspections across the Lisbon or Porto metropolitan area (apartments, houses, villas)
- Produce detailed technical reports using the InspectOS digital platform within 48 hours of site visit
- Identify and classify building defects across structural, humidity, electrical, gas, covering, urbanism, and systems categories
- Reference applicable Portuguese regulations (DL 10/2024, RTIEBT, DL 97/2017, DL 101-D/2020) in report findings
- Issue Energy Performance Certificates (EPC) if ADENE-qualified

**Quality & Leadership (25%)**
- Mentor and evaluate trainee/junior inspectors during their 90-day onboarding journey
- Conduct trial inspections for new contractor candidates using the InspectOS 100-point evaluation rubric
- Perform random quality audits on reports produced by other inspectors
- Maintain OE professional liability insurance and continuing education credits

**Client & Business (15%)**
- Communicate professionally with clients during inspections, explaining findings in accessible language
- Support Business Development with expert input on B2B partnerships (real estate agencies, banks, insurers)
- Contribute to InspectOS knowledge base and training materials

## Requirements

### Essential
- **Active OE registration** (Ordem dos Engenheiros) in Civil Engineering, with **signing authority** (Nível 1, Nível 2, Sénior, or Especialista)
- **Active professional liability insurance declaration** from Ageas Portugal (€75,000 coverage) — or willingness to obtain immediately
- **Minimum 3 years** of professional experience in construction, building pathology, or property inspection
- **Valid Portuguese driver's license** and own vehicle (essential for site visits)
- **Portuguese language fluency** (written and spoken) — reports must be court-admissible in Portuguese
- **Residence in Lisbon or Porto metropolitan area** (or willingness to relocate)

### Desirable
- **ADENE assessor qualification** (EPC issuance capability)
- Experience with **thermal imaging, moisture meters, or diagnostic equipment**
- **English fluency** for international client work
- Prior experience in **building inspection, pathology assessment, or construction supervision**
- Familiarity with **digital tools and platforms** — we provide full training on the InspectOS system

# Position 2: Operations & Compliance Manager
### Lisbon | Full-Time (Contrato sem Termo)

## About the Role

The Operations & Compliance Manager is the **organizational backbone** of InspectOS. You will orchestrate the day-to-day running of our inspection operation — managing a growing network of full-time and contractor inspectors, ensuring scheduling efficiency, enforcing quality standards, and maintaining regulatory compliance across OE, DGEG/ADENE, and IMPIC requirements.

This is a **foundational hire** for our scaling phase. You will report directly to the CEO and have significant autonomy to build the operational systems that will support InspectOS as we grow from 10 to 30+ inspectors over the next 18 months.

## Key Responsibilities

**Inspector Network Management (35%)**
- Manage the full inspector lifecycle: recruitment coordination, onboarding execution, performance tracking, and retention
- Maintain relationships with 10–20 active inspectors (employees + contractors) across Lisbon and Porto
- Handle inspector queries, disputes, and escalations
- Plan regional coverage and capacity for peak demand periods

**Scheduling & Dispatch (25%)**
- Manage the inspection booking pipeline: assign inspections to appropriate inspectors based on location, expertise, and availability
- Optimize inspector routes to minimize travel time and maximize daily inspection capacity
- Handle client rescheduling, cancellations, and urgent requests
- Monitor SLA compliance (48-hour report delivery target)

**Quality Assurance & Compliance (25%)**
- Conduct random report audits using the InspectOS 100-point quality rubric
- Verify that all inspectors maintain **active OE registration** and **professional liability insurance** (monthly checks)
- Track ADENE certification status for EPC-issuing inspectors
- Maintain regulatory compliance documentation for OE, DGEG, ADENE, and IMPIC
- Coordinate corrective actions for quality failures or client complaints
- Serve as the **internal Data Protection liaison** — coordinate with our external DPO on GDPR matters

**Training & Process (10%)**
- Execute the 90-day onboarding journey for new inspectors (coordinate shadowing, trial inspections, independence assessments)
- Maintain and update the InspectOS Knowledge Base (SOPs, regulations, best practices)
- Identify process improvements and efficiency gains
- Organize monthly team meetups and quarterly all-hands events

**Reporting & Metrics (5%)**
- Produce weekly operations dashboards: inspection volume, quality scores, client satisfaction, coverage gaps
- Track key metrics: time-to-hire, cost-per-inspection, inspector retention, SLA compliance

- **English fluency** — startup environment with international elements

- **Residence in Lisbon** (or willingness to relocate)

# Position 3: External Legal Counsel (Retainer-Based)
### Lisbon / Remote | Part-Time / Retainer

## About the Engagement

InspectOS produces **court-admissible technical reports** that carry professional liability, operates a platform handling personal data subject to GDPR, and navigates a complex regulatory landscape spanning the Ordem dos Engenheiros (OE), Direção-Geral de Energia e Geologia (DGEG), ADENE, IMPIC, and the evolving Simplex Urbanístico framework (DL 10/2024).

We seek a **Portuguese-registered lawyer** with expertise in **real estate law, construction/engineering law, and commercial contracts** to serve as our external legal counsel on a **monthly retainer basis**.

This is a **strategic advisory role**, not a litigation role. You will be our legal brain trust — ensuring that our contracts protect us, our reports are defensible, and our regulatory compliance is bulletproof as we scale.

## Scope of Work

**Contracts & Commercial (40%)**
- Draft and review **employment contracts** (full-time, fixed-term, internship)
- Draft and review **contractor service agreements** (recibo verde) with quality SLAs, IP clauses, and liability allocation
- Draft and review **client Terms & Conditions** for inspection services
- Draft and review **partnership MOUs** (real estate agencies, banks, insurers, builder warranties)
- Advise on **non-compete and confidentiality clauses** compliant with Portuguese labor law

**Regulatory & Compliance (30%)**
- Monitor regulatory changes affecting property inspection (DL 10/2024 implementation, RGEU replacement, EPBD Directive 2024/1275 transposition)
- Advise on **OE compliance requirements** for inspection report validity
- Advise on **IMPIC obligations** including anti-money-laundering (AML) requirements for real estate-related activities
- Review report **disclaimers and liability limitations** for legal defensibility
- Advise on **data protection compliance** in coordination with our external DPO

**Risk & Liability (20%)**
- Advise on **professional liability exposure** for inspection reports
- Review **insurance adequacy** (OE group policy + corporate liability)
- Advise on **client dispute resolution** and complaint handling
- Assist with **regulatory inquiries** or challenges to report validity

**Corporate (10%)**
- Support **fundraising documentation** (term sheets, shareholder agreements) as needed
- Advise on **international expansion** legal structure (Brazilian/Angolan market entry)
- Corporate governance matters as they arise

### Essential

- **Specialization in at least two of**: real estate law, construction law, engineering professional liability, commercial contracts
- **Portuguese fluency** (native or near-native) — all regulatory and legal work is in Portuguese
- **English fluency** — startup environment, occasional international elements
- **Lisbon-based** or able to attend in-person meetings in Lisbon monthly

- Familiarity with the **Ordem dos Engenheiros** regulatory framework
- Experience with **GDPR compliance** for digital platforms
