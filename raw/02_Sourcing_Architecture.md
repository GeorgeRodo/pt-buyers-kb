# InspectOS Sourcing Channel Architecture

## TL;DR

This document designs a **7-channel sourcing system** for InspectOS with a **Sourcing Matrix** mapping each channel to target profiles, expected volume, cost per hire, and time-to-first-inspection. The three **"Fast Win" channels** capable of producing candidates within 14 days are: **(1) OE Direct** (post on the Bolsa de Emprego immediately), **(2) Freelance/Contractor Direct Outreach** (contact individual OE-registered inspectors already operating as sole practitioners), and **(3) InspectOS Careers Page + Indeed.pt** (free posting with paid promotion). The **90-day launch sequence** activates OE Direct and job boards in **Week 1**, university partnerships (FEUP, IST, UC) in **Week 2**, Brazilian CREA-to-OE sourcing in **Week 4**, IEFP internship postings in **Week 8**, and agency partnerships in **Week 12**. Total 90-day budget estimate: **€8,500–€12,000** across all channels. Every channel includes a **concrete next action** — contact name, URL, form, or phone number — with no theoretical channels.

## 1. Channel 1: Ordem dos Engenheiros Direct

### 1.1 Overview and Strategic Rationale

The **Ordem dos Engenheiros (OE)** is not merely a professional regulator — it is the single most concentrated repository of qualified inspection talent in Portugal. With **64,261 members** (of whom **30,499 are civil engineers**) [^23^], the OE's **Bolsa de Emprego** (Job Bank) provides direct access to active job seekers and passive browsers within the exact profile InspectOS requires: OE-registered engineers with legal authority to sign court-admissible inspection reports. This channel is rated **Tier 1 Priority** because it combines unmatched targeting precision (100% OE-registered audience) with zero direct cost and immediate activation potential.

### 1.2 Access Mechanisms

The OE operates **two parallel job platforms** that InspectOS should use simultaneously:

| **Platform** | **URL** | **Access Method** | **Cost** | **Audience** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bolsa de Emprego OE (National) | [bolsaemprego.ordemdosengenheiros.pt](https://bolsaemprego.ordemdosengenheiros.pt) | OE member login or employer registration | Free for OE members / partner employers | All 64,261 OE members |
| OET Job Board (Engineering Technicians) | [oet.pt/portal/index.php/bolsa-de-emprego](https://oet.pt/portal/index.php/bolsa-de-emprego/oferta-de-emprego) | Employer registration | Free | OET-registered technicians |
| OE Regional North | [oern.pt](https://www.oern.pt) | Direct contact with regional council | Free | Norte region members (Porto) |
| OE Regional South | [oe-sul.pt](https://www.oe-sul.pt) | Direct contact with regional council | Free | Sul region members (Lisboa) |

The **Bolsa de Emprego** currently hosts **100+ active job listings** [^26^], with new postings added daily across civil, electrotechnical, mechanical, and environmental engineering specializations. [^72^] Posting requires either an **OE membership login** (for individual members posting as employers) or **employer partner registration**. For InspectOS, the fastest path is to register as an employer partner through the national OE portal.

### 1.3 Exact Process and Contacts

**Step-by-step posting process:**

1. **Register as an employer** at [bolsaemprego.ordemdosengenheiros.pt](https://bolsaemprego.ordemdosengenheiros.pt) — requires company NIF (Portuguese tax ID), which InspectOS already has.
2. **Create the job posting** with required fields: job title, location (Lisbon/Porto/remote), specialty (Engenharia Civil), contract type (full-time / contractor / internship), and detailed description.
3. **Submit for moderation** — OE staff reviews postings typically within **24–48 hours**.
4. **Posting goes live** to all OE members with matching specialty alerts.

## 3. Channel 3: Brazilian CREA-to-OE Funnel

### 3.1 Overview and Strategic Rationale

With **500,000 Brazilians already resident in Portugal** [^17^] and the **CREA-to-OE reciprocity pathway** eliminating diploma revalidation requirements, Brazil represents InspectOS's **highest-potential international sourcing channel**. This channel is differentiated because most Portuguese employers are unaware of or underutilize the reciprocity agreement, giving InspectOS a first-mover advantage in attracting Brazilian civil engineers who are already in Portugal or considering relocation.

### 3.2 Sourcing Workflow Design

The Brazilian funnel operates in **two tiers**: (1) **Brazilians already in Portugal** (immediate hiring potential), and (2) **Brazilians in Brazil considering Portugal** (medium-term pipeline). Each tier requires different platforms and messaging.

**Tier 1: Brazilians Already in Portugal**

| **Platform** | **URL** | **Approach** | **Expected Response** |
|---|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn (Portuguese filters) | linkedin.com/talent | Search "Engenheiro Civil" + "Portugal" + "Brasil" | High — passive candidates visible |
| Facebook Groups | "Brasileiros em Portugal", "Engenheiros no Portugal" | Community engagement + job posts | Medium — high trust, word-of-mouth |
| CREA State Chapters | crea-sp.org.br, crea-rj.org.br, etc. | Direct partnership outreach | High — institutional credibility |
| Brazilian Community Associations | Casa do Brasil (Lisbon), others | Event sponsorship + job board posting | Medium — community trust |

**Tier 2: Brazilians in Brazil Considering Portugal**

| **Platform** | **URL** | **Approach** |
|---|---|---|
| Vagas.com | vagas.com.br | Post in Portuguese highlighting Portugal opportunity |
| Indeed Brasil | indeed.com.br | Cross-post Lisbon/Porto positions |
| LinkedIn Brasil | linkedin.com | Target "open to relocation" civil engineers |
| CREA National (CONFEA) | confea.org.br | Request dissemination to state chapters |
| Jornal de Engenharia (CONFEA) | Professional publication | Advertise or editorial placement |

### 3.3 Pre-Qualification Protocol

Before a Brazilian candidate enters InspectOS's vetting pipeline, they must be pre-qualified for the CREA-to-OE reciprocity pathway:

| **Pre-Qualification Check** | **Verification Method** | **Pass Criteria** |
|---|---|---|
| Active CREA registration | [sica.crea.gov.br](https://sica.crea.gov.br) online verification | Registration status "Ativo" and "Adimplente" |
| No ethical sanctions | Request "certidão negativa" from CREA | No sanctions in past 5 years |
| Coursework hours | Academic transcript review | Minimum 3,600 hours in engineering degree |
| Brazilian nationality | Passport copy | Native or naturalized Brazilian |
| Excluded profession check | Review degree title | Not tecnólogo, técnico, geólogo, geógrafo, or meteorologista |

### 3.4 Messaging Framework

The value proposition for Brazilian engineers must address their specific concerns:

- **"Exerça como engenheiro em Portugal sem revalidar diploma"** (Practice as an engineer in Portugal without revalidating your degree) — headline message emphasizing the reciprocity advantage.
- **"CREA → OEP em 90 dias, custo de R$ 59,42"** — specific timeline and low cost reduce perceived barriers.
- **"500 mil brasileiros já estão em Portugal"** — social proof that the migration path is well-trodden.
- **"Inspeção imobiliária: especialidade em crescimento com demanda garantida"** — positions inspection as a growth niche with stable demand.

### 3.5 Performance Expectations

| **Metric** | **Expected Value** |
|---|---|
| Time to first candidate | 14–21 days |
| Cost per hire | €200–€500 (platform + sponsorship costs) |
| Candidate quality signal | High (CREA pre-qualification filters for qualified engineers) |
| Expected applications (90 days) | 10–20 |
| Conversion to trial inspection | 30–40% (self-selected, motivated candidates) |

## 4. Channel 4: Angolan Practical Talent

### 4.1 Overview and Strategic Rationale

The Angolan channel is best approached not as a direct OE registration pathway (which faces the full **DGES degree recognition burden** of 6–12+ months and €2,000–€5,000 in costs) [^18^][^19^], but as a **contractor support talent pool** of experienced site engineers who can perform field inspections under the direct supervision of an OE-registered senior inspector. Angola's large infrastructure boom over the past two decades has produced civil engineers with deep practical experience in **concrete pathology, structural assessment, and tropical climate building defects** — skills that are directly transferable to Portuguese inspection work.

### 4.2 Realistic Sourcing Path

| **Source** | **Mechanism** | **Contact Approach** |
|---|---|---|
| Teixeira Duarte Angola rotation | 37% of TD workforce in Angola [^56^]; engineers rotate to Portugal | Contact TD Angola HR for referral partnerships |
| Mota-Engil Angola operations | Major Portuguese contractor active in Angola | LinkedIn outreach to Angolan-based Portuguese engineers |
| Angolan diaspora in Portugal | Community networks in Lisbon (Amadora, Queluz) | Community association engagement |
| OEA (Ordem dos Engenheiros de Angola) members | Professional network; no reciprocity but individual recognition possible | Direct outreach to OEA-registered engineers in Portugal |
| Construction site networks | Angolan site engineers working on Portuguese projects | Word-of-mouth through construction industry contacts |

### 4.3 Legal Structure for Support Roles

Angolan engineers working as contractor support must operate under **direct OE supervision**: an OE-registered senior inspector must sign all reports, and the supervision ratio must comply with OE professional liability requirements. The Angolan contractor contributes **field observation, photography, measurement, and preliminary documentation**, while the OE-registered senior provides **technical judgment, report authorship, and legal responsibility**.

| **Role** | **Requirements** | **Compensation** |
|---|---|---|
| Field Support Inspector (Angolan) | Site experience, construction knowledge, Portuguese language | €150–€200 per inspection day |
| OE-Registered Lead Inspector | OE civil engineering registration, signing authority | €250–€350 per inspection day |

For a senior inspector at €40,000/year, this translates to **€6,000–€8,000** in agency fees. Given InspectOS's current hiring volume (1–2 full-time + 5 contractors), agency partnerships should be approached on a **contingency basis only** (no retained search fees) and limited to **1–2 agencies** to avoid fee duplication.

2. **Engage Xelvin or PROMAN** for contractor/technical roles (both have engineering specialization and Porto presence).
