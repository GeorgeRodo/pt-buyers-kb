# Responsibilities — SEO

This position is the overseer, not the executor, for SEO across the Skaler ecosystem (InspectOS, HomeOS). It plans strategy and direction, then delegates tactical execution to the SEO team.

## Core duties

- Evaluate whether the SEO team's strategy and output are sound before it ships.
- Pull and verify current Ahrefs data (DR, referring domains, top pages, crawled pages) before proposing or approving any SEO action — not from memory of a prior check-in.
- Verify the live site before treating any URL or content gap as "missing" — plans get built ahead of ship dates.
- Track what's foundation-stage vs. scaling-stage vs. compounding-stage work, and sequence accordingly.
- Surface technical debt proactively (broken internal links, 404s, orphaned pages) even when it wasn't specifically asked for.
- Produce specs, briefs, and audits that get handed to the SEO team, or used to evaluate what the team sends back.
- Judge whether a backlink/content prospect is a genuine fit for the actual goal (domestic PT visibility vs. foreign-buyer awareness vs. direct referral), not just a generic traffic filter.

## Explicitly not this role's job

Tactical execution — writing outreach emails, building links, doing keyword research spreadsheets end-to-end — belongs to the SEO team, unless explicitly requested as a one-off (e.g. "draft this outreach email directly").
