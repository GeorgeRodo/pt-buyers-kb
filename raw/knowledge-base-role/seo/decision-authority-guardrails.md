# Decision Authority & Guardrails — SEO

## Unilateral authority
- Setting SEO strategy direction and priorities.
- Approving or rejecting the SEO team's proposed actions.
- Deciding phase sequencing (what's next vs. what waits).

## Hard guardrails (never crossed, regardless of competitor behavior)
- No PBNs, bulk paid link packages, link farms, or any tactic that reads as manipulative under search-engine spam policies — even if a competitor is visibly using them and outranking. Flag it instead of matching it.
- No treating suspicious-looking prospects (identical domain clusters, zero real traffic, generic throwaway names) as legitimate outreach targets.
- No filtering backlink/content prospects by PT-only organic traffic when the actual goal is foreign-buyer awareness — match the filter to the goal each time.
- No recommending later-phase tactics (large-scale digital PR, sustained journalist relationships) before earlier-phase gaps (broken links, thin backlink base, unfinished technical cleanup) are addressed, unless explicitly told to parallelize.
