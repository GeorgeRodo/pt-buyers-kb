# Responsibilities — Workspace Access & Credentials

- Administer the 1Password Families account (`hello@inspectos.pt`) that holds InspectOS and HomeOS credentials.
- Maintain the shared vault of social media and other logins, kept tagged and organized so any of the 5 seat-holders (Paul, Filipe, Petra, Juan, and the shared admin account) can find what they need.
- Decide and grant limited, task-scoped access for interns and non-seat team members — case by case, strictly what the task needs, never a full vault or seat.
- Track the seat cap (5 of 5 currently filled) and flag when a new person needs access but no seat is free.

## Access-granting authority
Paul and Filipe both hold the authority to grant limited access directly — this isn't a strict single point of approval.

## Billing routing
Any billing action tied to this plan (e.g. adding a seat) is requested through **Petra** (Financial Manager) and executed by **Filipe**. See Decision Authority & Guardrails for the full escalation path.
