## Shared vault contents

- A shared vault holds social media logins and other credentials for **InspectOS** and **HomeOS**, organized with tags.
- Visible to the 5 seat-holders above.

## Intern / non-seat access

- Interns and other temporary team members are **not** given a named seat (none available).
- They're granted **limited, item-level access** — strictly the specific credential(s) their task requires, not the full vault.
- This can be provisioned by Paul or Filipe.
