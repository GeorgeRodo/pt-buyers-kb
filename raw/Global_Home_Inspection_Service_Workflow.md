# Global Home Inspection Service Workflow

Based on research into leading inspection platforms and industry best practices, here is the complete end-to-end workflow for home inspection services as operated by companies worldwide.

---

## Phase 1: Inquiry & Booking

### 1. Lead Capture
- Inquiries arrive via website forms, phone, real estate agent referrals, or direct buyer contact
- AI receptionists or CRM systems log and categorize leads immediately
- Integration with listing platforms (where available) auto-populates property details

### 2. Scope Definition & Quoting
- Inspector or system determines inspection type: pre-purchase, pre-listing, new construction/snagging, warranty, or specialty (radon, mold, termite, sewer scope)
- Pricing calculated based on property size, age, location, and add-on services
- Typical add-ons: radon ($150–$250), sewer scope ($125–$400), termite/WDI ($50–$280), mold ($300–$600)

### 3. Scheduling & Confirmation
- Self-service booking portals allow 24/7 appointment selection
- Automated calendar sync prevents double-booking
- Drive-time buffers and inspector availability optimized by route
- Instant confirmations via email/SMS with appointment reminders
- Pre-inspection agreements sent for digital signature

---

## Phase 2: Pre-Inspection Preparation

### 4. Data Collection
- Inspector reviews property records, seller disclosures, previous inspection history
- Municipal data pulled for permit history, code violations, zoning
- Satellite imagery and GIS data reviewed for site context
- Client-specific concerns or focus areas noted

### 5. Equipment & Team Preparation
- Inspection kit prepared: moisture meters, thermal cameras, electrical testers, drones (for roofs), gas detectors
- For team operations: assignment distributed, inspector credentials verified
- Route optimization for multi-inspection days

---

## Phase 3: On-Site Inspection

### 6. Arrival & Setup
- Inspector arrives, introduces process to client/buyer (if present)
- Mobile/tablet-based inspection software loaded with property-specific template
- Pre-inspection walkthrough with client to identify specific concerns

### 7. Systematic Examination
Standard inspection covers: structure, roof, electrical, plumbing, HVAC, exterior, interior, insulation, ventilation
- Duration: 2–4 hours for typical single-family home
- Photo/video evidence captured with GPS metadata and annotations
- Defects logged with severity ratings, location data, and immediate notes
- Voice-to-text or AI-assisted comment generation for efficiency
- Offline capability ensures continuous work in poor connectivity areas

### 8. Inspector Walkthrough Summary
- On-site debrief with client highlighting critical findings
- Immediate safety concerns communicated verbally
- No formal report yet—this is preliminary orientation

---

## Phase 4: Reporting & Quality Control

### 9. Report Generation
- Field data auto-populates into structured templates
- AI tools suggest narrative comments, severity classifications, and recommendations
- Photo evidence linked to specific findings with location metadata
- Executive summary auto-generated highlighting major defects
- Inspector review and sign-off before finalization

### 10. Quality Assurance
- Supervisor review triggered for complex cases or trainee inspectors
- Compliance check against industry standards (ASHI, InterNACHI, or local equivalents)
- Version control and audit trail maintained
- Report locked to prevent post-delivery edits

### 11. Report Delivery
- Delivery within 24–48 hours (same-day with modern software)
- Formats: branded PDF, interactive web portal, or mobile-friendly digital report
- Distribution to: buyer, buyer's agent, sometimes seller/seller's agent (with permission)
- Secure client portals for access and sharing

---

## Phase 5: Post-Inspection Client Engagement

### 12. Report Review Support
- Inspector available for phone/video call to walk through findings
- Clarification on technical terminology, severity ratings, and implications
- Guidance on prioritizing repairs and maintenance

### 13. Negotiation Support (Pre-Purchase Context)
- Findings triaged into: Safety Issues, Structural/Major System Defects, Maintenance Items
- Repair request templates tied to report language and photo evidence
- Specialist referrals (structural engineers, roofing contractors, electricians) for further evaluation
- Timeline pressure: repair requests typically submitted within 5–10 days of report receipt to preserve contingency options

---

## Phase 6: Business Operations & Follow-Up

### 14. Invoicing & Payment
- Automated invoicing triggered upon report delivery
- Digital payment processing (credit card, bank transfer)
- "Pay-at-close" options integrated with title companies in some markets
- Payment reminders for outstanding balances

### 15. Data Management & Analytics
- Inspection records archived in centralized database
- Performance metrics tracked: inspection volume, revenue, client trends, agent activity
- Trend analysis for market intelligence and service improvement

### 16. Follow-Up & Retention
- Automated maintenance reminders based on inspection findings
- Annual inspection offers
- Referral program activation
- Review requests for online reputation management

---

## Phase 7: Continuous Improvement

### 17. Feedback Loop
- Client satisfaction surveys post-inspection
- Inspector performance reviews
- Template refinement based on common findings or regulatory changes

### 18. Regulatory & Compliance Updates
- Standards updated to reflect new building codes, energy regulations (EPC requirements), or liability frameworks
- Inspector continuing education tracked
- Certification maintenance (ASHI, InterNACHI, state licenses)

---

## Key Workflow Variations by Market

| Market Characteristic | Workflow Adaptation |
|---|---|
| **US/Canada** | Heavy agent-mediated scheduling; contingency timelines drive urgency; standardized ASHI/InterNACHI checklists; pay-at-close common |
| **UK/Australia** | Snagging inspections for new builds prominent; RICS standards; more direct consumer booking |
| **Northern Europe** | Energy performance certificates (EPC) integrated; regulatory compliance emphasis; digital-first reporting |
| **Emerging Markets** | Less standardized; higher need for education; often cash-based; limited municipal data availability |
| **Portugal (Your Context)** | Fragmented data landscape; deregulation trends; DL 108/2026 creating new inspection obligations; need for dual-language reporting; opportunity to establish standards before consolidation |

## Technology Stack Enabling This Workflow

Modern inspection companies typically operate on platforms combining:
- **Scheduling automation** (Cal.com, proprietary portals)
- **Mobile field apps** (offline-capable, photo-annotating)
- **AI-assisted reporting** (comment generation, image defect detection)
- **CRM/lead management** (automated follow-up, agent relationship tracking)
- **Payment processing** (integrated invoicing, pay-at-close)
- **Client portals** (secure report access, sharing capabilities)
- **Analytics dashboards** (business intelligence, trend tracking)

Leading platforms referenced: ISN (Inspection Support Network), Tap Inspect, Property Inspect, HomeInspecto, InspectPro HQ, and LEADSORBIT CRM.
