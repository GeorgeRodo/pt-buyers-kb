## THE PROBLEM

Why Property Condition Is Now Part of Your Adequacy Exposure

---

### The Regulatory Hook

DL 81-C/2017 Art. 45: You must only intermediate contracts on which you have "informacao detalhada e objetiva." A property with hidden structural defects creates costs the consumer does not know about.

### The Appraisal Gap

The bank appraisal values the property. It does not verify structural integrity, MEP condition, energy reality, or legal compliance. A 350,000 EUR appraisal may miss 45,000 EUR in undisclosed repairs.

### The Macroprudential Reality

Banco de Portugal data: LTV at 99% for guaranteed loans, DSTI at 24-30%, maturity at 31+ years. Portuguese borrowers are stretched. Hidden repair costs push them into default.
