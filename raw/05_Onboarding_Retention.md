# InspectOS Field Team Operations Manual

## TL;DR

This manual designs a **scalable onboarding, training, and retention system** for InspectOS's hybrid field team. The **90-day onboarding journey** progresses from compliance and shadowing (Days 1–14) through supervised solo inspections (Days 15–30) to independence assessment (Days 60–90). The **training curriculum** covers five core modules: InspectOS reporting standards, Portuguese building code essentials, DGEG/ADENE energy certification integration, client communication, and digital tool mastery. The **Quality Assurance Loop** mandates random audits at frequencies of **100%** (trial inspections), **25%** (Month 1–3), **15%** (Month 4–12), and **10%** (Senior inspectors), with peer review for all reports scoring below 85%. The **retention target** is **85% at 12 months** for full-time employees and **70% at 12 months** for contractors — achieved through a defined career path (Inspector → Senior → Team Lead → Regional Manager), OE continuing education sponsorship, quality bonuses, and autonomy. For distributed field teams where daily in-person contact is impossible, the system relies on **weekly video standups**, **monthly in-person team meetings**, **real-time Slack/WhatsApp coordination**, and a **peer recognition program** to maintain cohesion.

## 1. Onboarding Journey (0–90 Days)

### 1.1 Overview: Four-Phase Structure

The onboarding journey is designed to bring four distinct candidate profiles — **OE veterans, Brazilian transfers, Angolan support engineers, and fresh graduates** — to a common standard of inspection competence within 90 days. Each phase has specific deliverables, evaluation criteria, and escalation triggers.

| **Phase** | **Duration** | **Focus** | **Deliverable** | **Go/No-Go Criteria** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1: Immersion** | Days 1–14 | Compliance, tools, brand, shadowing | 3+ completed shadow inspections; platform proficiency | OE insurance verified; platform login active; shadow evaluations ≥80% |
| **Phase 2: Supervised Solo** | Days 15–30 | Supervised independent inspections | 5+ supervised solo inspections with real-time feedback | All reports submitted within 48h; quality score ≥70%; no client complaints |
| **Phase 3: Quality Calibration** | Days 31–60 | Independent work with audit | 10+ independent inspections; first random audit | Quality audit ≥80%; client satisfaction ≥4.0/5.0; volume target met |
| **Phase 4: Independence Assessment** | Days 61–90 | Full independence; career pathing | 15+ total inspections; independence sign-off | Quality audit ≥85%; zero substantiated complaints; career discussion completed |

### 1.2 Phase 1: Immersion (Days 1–14)

**Day 1–3: Compliance, Tools, and Brand Immersion**

| **Activity** | **Duration** | **Owner** | **Deliverable** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Welcome session + team introduction | 2 hours | Operations Manager | Signed welcome pack; team introductions |
| OE insurance verification | 1 hour | HR/Operations | Insurance declaration filed; copy retained |
| InspectOS platform training | 4 hours | Senior Inspector | Platform login; template navigation; photo upload; GPS logging |
| Brand values and client promise | 2 hours | Founder/CEO | Understanding of InspectOS market positioning and quality standards [^121^] |
| Report template walkthrough | 3 hours | Senior Inspector | Familiarity with 100+ checkpoint structure; severity grading system [^121^] |
| Digital tool setup (Slack, WhatsApp, email) | 1 hour | Operations | Active on all communication channels |
| Equipment issuance (measuring tools, tablet if needed) | 1 hour | Operations | Signed equipment receipt |

**Day 4–14: Shadowing Protocol**

The new hire shadows **two different senior inspectors** across a minimum of **3 inspections** each (6 total), observing different property types and inspection styles.

| **Shadow Element** | **What to Observe** | **Evaluation Point** |
|---|---|---|
| **Pre-inspection preparation** | Route planning, client communication, document review | Does the candidate ask relevant questions about property history? |
| **On-site systematic approach** | Room-by-room methodology, tool usage, photo documentation | Does the candidate follow a logical sequence? Are photos clear and comprehensive? |
| **Client interaction** | Professional demeanor, explanation of process, managing expectations | Is the candidate comfortable with client-facing communication? |
| **Defect identification** | What the senior inspector notices; what they document vs. what they verbally note | Does the candidate spot the same defects? Do they understand severity classification? |
| **Report completion** | Template usage, writing style, regulatory references, timeline | Can the candidate complete a report section independently after shadowing? |
| **Post-inspection workflow** | Photo organization, report submission, invoice/recibo verde issuance (contractors) | Does the candidate understand the full workflow? |

**Shadowing Evaluation Form:** After each shadow inspection, the senior inspector completes a **10-question evaluation** (scored 1–5) covering technical observation, communication, organization, and enthusiasm. The candidate must average **≥4.0** across all shadow inspections to proceed to Phase 2.

**Differences for Profile Types:**

| **Profile** | **Phase 1 Modification** |
|---|---|
| **OE Veteran** | Accelerated tool training; focus on InspectOS-specific template and process differences from their previous experience |
| **Brazilian Transfer** | Additional Portuguese regulatory context (DL 10/2024, RSEI, RCCTE); cultural orientation; OEP registration status tracking |
| **Angolan Support** | Intensive Portuguese building code training; supervised field work only (no report signing); focus on documentation and photography |
| **Fresh Graduate** | Extended shadowing (minimum 8 inspections); foundational building pathology training; mentorship pairing |

### 1.3 Phase 2: Supervised Solo (Days 15–30)

The candidate conducts **5 supervised solo inspections** — they lead the inspection while a senior inspector observes and provides real-time feedback. The senior inspector does not intervene unless the candidate misses a critical safety issue or significantly mishandles client interaction.

| **Inspection #** | **Property Type** | **Senior Inspector Role** | **Feedback Method** |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Standard apartment (T1–T2) | Shadow; observe only | Detailed debrief after each inspection |
| 3–4 | Apartment (T3+) or small house | Shadow; light intervention if needed | Real-time coaching + written feedback |
| 5 | Complex property or diagnostic | Shadow; evaluate for Phase 3 gate | Comprehensive evaluation; go/no-go decision |

**Real-Time Feedback Protocol:**
- **Immediately post-inspection (15 min):** Quick verbal debrief — what went well, 2–3 improvement areas
- **Within 24 hours:** Written feedback on report quality using standardized rubric
- **End of Week 2:** Mid-phase check-in — progress review, concerns, adjustments

### 1.4 Phase 3: Quality Calibration (Days 31–60)

The candidate conducts **10 independent inspections** with random audit by senior inspectors. This phase tests whether the candidate can maintain quality without direct supervision.

**Random Audit Process:**
- 25% of inspections in this phase are randomly selected for **unannounced audit**
- Senior inspector reviews report within 48 hours of submission
- Audit covers: defect detection completeness, severity accuracy, regulatory references, photo quality, writing clarity
- Audit score of **≥80%** required to maintain independent status; scores of 65–79% trigger additional supervised inspections; scores below 65% trigger retraining

### 1.5 Phase 4: Independence Assessment (Days 61–90)

| **Milestone** | **Requirement** | **Outcome** |
|---|---|---|
| **Volume target** | 15+ total inspections completed | Confirms productivity |
| **Quality audit** | ≥85% average on random audits | Confirms quality consistency |
| **Client satisfaction** | ≥4.0/5.0 average rating | Confirms client-facing competence |
| **Zero substantiated complaints** | No validated client complaints | Confirms professionalism |
| **Career path discussion** | Completed with Operations Manager | Sets expectations for growth |
| **Independence sign-off** | Signed by Operations Manager + Senior Inspector | **Full inspector status achieved** |

**90-Day Onboarding Calendar Template:**

| **Week** | **Focus** | **Key Activities** | **Evaluation** |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Compliance & Tools | Platform training; brand immersion; equipment setup | OE insurance verified; platform test passed |
| 2 | Shadowing | 3 inspections with Senior A; 3 with Senior B | Shadow evaluations ≥80% |
| 3 | Supervised Solo | 3 solo inspections with senior present | Reports submitted on time; quality ≥70% |
| 4 | Supervised Solo + Feedback | 2 solo inspections; mid-phase review | Go/No-Go for Phase 3 |
| 5 | Independent + Audit | 3 independent inspections; 1 random audit | Audit ≥80% |
| 6 | Independent + Audit | 3 independent inspections; 1 random audit | Audit ≥80% |
| 7 | Volume + Calibration | 3 independent inspections; peer review | Volume target on track |
| 8 | Quality Focus | 2 inspections; deep-dive quality review | Quality trend analysis |
| 9 | Independence Prep | 2 inspections; mock client scenario | Client communication assessment |
| 10 | Independence Assessment | Final audit; career discussion; sign-off | Independence sign-off or extension |
| 11–12 | Full Integration | Full inspection load; team integration | Normal operations |

## 2. Training Curriculum

### 2.1 Module 1: InspectOS Reporting Standards and Template Mastery

**Objective:** Every inspector produces reports that are consistent, complete, and instantly recognizable as InspectOS quality.

| **Topic** | **Content** | **Duration** | **Assessment** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Report template structure | 100+ checkpoint system; 6 severity categories; bilingual EN/PT format [^121^] | 4 hours | Complete sample report |
| Severity classification | Critical → Functional → Significant → Moderate → Minor → Cosmetic [^121^] | 2 hours | Classify 20 sample defects |
| Photo documentation standards | Minimum shots per room; angle requirements; labeling convention | 2 hours | Photo portfolio review |
| 48-hour delivery standard | Workflow optimization; time management; deadline management | 1 hour | Commitment acknowledgment |
| Digital report generation | Platform usage; auto-population; manual entry; review and submit | 4 hours | Platform proficiency test |

### 2.2 Module 2: Portuguese Building Code Essentials

**Objective:** Inspectors can identify regulatory non-compliance and reference applicable Portuguese law in their reports.

| **Regulation** | **Relevance to Inspection** | **Training Focus** |
|---|---|---|
| **DL 10/2024 (Simplex Urbanístico)** [^114^][^118^] | Eliminated licença de utilização requirement; shifted liability to buyers; increased inspection demand | Explain to clients why inspection is now essential; reference in marketing materials |
| **Lei 31/2009** [^116^] | Qualification requirements for technical construction roles; engineer responsibilities | Understand professional liability boundaries |
| **RTIEBT** (Portaria 949-A/2006) | Electrical installation safety standards | Identify non-compliant panels, lack of differential protection, improper grounding |
| **DL 97/2017** | Gas installation safety; mandatory periodic inspection | Verify gas inspection certificates; identify visible safety issues |
| **DL 101-D/2020** [^138^] | Energy certification mandatory for sales/rentals | Issue or verify EPC; understand energy rating implications |
| **RGEU** (until June 2026) / New regime | General urban building regulations | Assess construction compliance; identify unregistered modifications |
| **RSEI** | Energy systems inspection in buildings | Evaluate HVAC efficiency; identify maintenance needs |
| **RCCTE** | Thermal behavior characteristics | Assess insulation quality; thermal bridge identification |

### 2.3 Module 3: DGEG/ADENE Energy Certification Integration

**Objective:** InspectOS inspectors can issue or verify Energy Performance Certificates as part of the inspection service.

The Portuguese Energy Certification System (SCE) is managed by **ADENE** under supervision of **DGEG**. [^136^][^137^] Qualified experts (architects or engineers with 5+ years experience, trained and assessed by ADENE) can issue EPCs. [^140^] The certificate rates properties from **A+ (most efficient) to F (least efficient)**. [^138^]

| **Training Element** | **Content** | **Duration** |
|---|---|---|
| SCE overview | ADENE's role; DGEG supervision; certificate validity (10 years residential) [^136^] | 2 hours |
| EPC calculation methodology | Thermal envelope assessment; system efficiency; energy consumption calculation [^136^] | 8 hours |
| SCE Portal usage | Registration; data entry; certificate issuance; database queries | 4 hours |
| ADENE quality requirements | 4–10% of certificates checked by ADENE; compliance criteria; non-consequence severity levels [^139^] | 2 hours |
| EPC integration with inspection | When to issue EPC during inspection; ADENE fee structure (€28–€65 residential) [^144^]; combining services | 2 hours |
| Indoor Air Quality (RSECE-QAI) | IAQ parameters; assessment methodology; certification process [^136^] | 4 hours |

**ADENE Expert Qualification Pathway:**
For inspectors who will issue EPCs, ADENE requires: [^136^][^140^]
- Architect or engineer with **minimum 5 years professional experience**
- Completion of **ADENE-approved training module**
- Pass **national assessor examination** (multiple choice + case study)
- **5-year registration** with renewal subject to continuing training

InspectOS should sponsor senior inspectors through this qualification, as EPC issuance is a **high-margin add-on service** (€150–€350 per certificate on the open market [^138^]).

## 3. Quality Assurance Loop

### 3.1 Random Audit Frequency by Seniority

| **Inspector Level** | **Audit Frequency** | **Audit Depth** | **Escalation Trigger** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Trial / Phase 1–2** | 100% (every inspection) | Full report review + photo review + client follow-up | Any score <70% = stop and retrain |
| **Newly Independent (0–3 months)** | 25% (1 in 4 inspections) | Full report review | Score <75% = additional supervised inspections |
| **Established (4–12 months)** | 15% (1 in 7 inspections) | Report review + spot photo check | Score <80% = warning + retraining; 2 consecutive <80% = probation |
| **Senior (12+ months)** | 10% (1 in 10 inspections) | Spot-check report review | Score <85% = peer review required; pattern of decline = performance review |

### 3.2 Peer Review Process

When an inspection report scores below 85% on audit, it triggers **peer review**:

1. A second senior inspector reviews the report independently
2. Both inspectors' findings are compared
3. Discrepancies are discussed in a structured review meeting
4. The original inspector receives detailed feedback
5. A follow-up audit is scheduled within 2 weeks

### 3.3 Client Feedback Integration

Every client receives a **post-inspection satisfaction survey** (3 questions, 30 seconds to complete):

1. "How would you rate the professionalism of your inspector?" (1–5 stars)
2. "How clear and useful was your inspection report?" (1–5 stars)
3. "Would you recommend InspectOS to a friend?" (Yes/No)

Scores are tracked per inspector and rolled into the **quality dashboard**.

### 3.4 Corrective Action Protocol

| **Issue Severity** | **Trigger** | **Action** | **Timeline** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Critical** | Safety hazard missed; report legally challenged; client threatens legal action | Immediate suspension from inspections; full investigation; retraining or termination | 24–48 hours |
| **Major** | Significant defect missed; report submitted >48h late; client complaint substantiated | Formal warning; mandatory retraining; 30-day probation with 50% audit frequency | 1 week |
| **Minor** | Formatting inconsistency; photo missing; minor writing error | Informal feedback; self-correction; noted for next audit | Next audit cycle |
| **Trend** | 2+ issues of same type within 30 days | Targeted retraining on specific skill gap | 1 week |

## 4. Retention System

### 4.1 Career Path

InspectOS provides a **clear, documented career progression** that gives engineers a reason to stay beyond compensation:

| **Level** | **Requirements** | **Compensation** | **Responsibilities** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Trainee / Intern** | Degree in progress or recent graduate; OE registration in process | IEFP stipend (€1,057/month) [^67^] | Shadowing; documentation; assisted inspections |
| **Junior Inspector** | OE registration; 6+ months experience; quality score ≥80% | €24,000–€28,000 FT OR €150–€200/inspection | Independent standard inspections |
| **Inspector** | 12+ months; quality score ≥85%; client satisfaction ≥4.2 | €28,000–€35,000 FT OR €200–€250/inspection | All inspection types; mentorship of trainees |
| **Senior Inspector** | 24+ months; quality score ≥90%; ADENE qualification; leadership | €35,000–€45,000 FT OR €250–€300/inspection | Complex diagnostics; trial inspection evaluation; quality audits |
| **Team Lead** | 36+ months; demonstrated leadership; 5+ inspectors managed | €45,000–€55,000 FT | Regional team management; client escalation; hiring input |
| **Regional Manager** | 48+ months; P&L responsibility; strategic planning | €55,000–€70,000 FT | Full regional P&L; expansion planning; partnership management |

### 4.2 OE Continuing Education Sponsorship

OE members must maintain **continuing education credits** to retain their titles. InspectOS sponsors this as a retention lever:

| **Sponsorship Element** | **Value** | **Rationale** |
|---|---|---|
| OE conference attendance | €500–€1,000/year | Professional development; networking; OE relationship building |
| ADENE assessor qualification course | €800–€1,500 one-time | Enables EPC issuance; high-margin service capability |
| Specialized training (concrete pathology, structural assessment) | €300–€800/course | Deepens expertise; improves inspection quality |
| IAQ certification (RSECE-QAI) | €500–€1,000 | Expands service offering; indoor air quality assessments |

### 4.3 Recognition and Incentive Programs

| **Program** | **Mechanism** | **Frequency** | **Value** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Inspector of the Month** | Highest quality score + client satisfaction | Monthly | €200 bonus + featured on team Slack |
| **Perfect Audit Streak** | 10 consecutive audits ≥90% | Ongoing | €500 bonus |
| **Client Champion** | Most 5-star ratings in a quarter | Quarterly | €300 bonus + certificate |
| **Mentorship Bonus** | Successfully onboard a new inspector to independence | Per onboarding | €250 bonus |
| **Referral Bonus** | Referred hire completes 90 days | Per referral | €500–€1,000 (tiered by role) |
| **Innovation Award** | Suggested process improvement implemented | Quarterly | €200 + recognition |

### 4.4 Autonomy and Flexibility

For contractors especially, **autonomy is a primary retention driver**. InspectOS maximizes this:

- **Self-scheduling:** Contractors choose which inspection requests to accept within their availability window
- **Geographic preference:** Contractors define their preferred service area (e.g., "Lisbon center only" or "Greater Porto")
- **No micromanagement:** Once independent, inspectors manage their own day; no check-ins required between inspections
- **Equipment choice:** Contractors use their own vehicles and phones; only specialized tools are provided on loan
- **Transparent payment:** Invoices paid within 14 days of report approval; payment status visible in platform

For distributed field teams, **intentional connection** is essential:

| **Ritual** | **Format** | **Frequency** | **Purpose** |

| **Monthly Team Meetup** | In-person lunch or coffee (Lisbon/Porto) | Monthly | Build relationships; share knowledge; team cohesion |
| **Quarterly All-Hands** | Half-day in-person (alternating Lisbon/Porto) | Quarterly | Business updates; strategy; team building |

## 6. Scalable Systems vs. Bespoke Hand-Holding

### 6.1 Principle: Document Everything

For a hybrid team (employees + contractors + partners) that will grow from 7 to 20+ inspectors, **bespoke hand-holding is unsustainable**. Every process must be documented, digitized, and self-serve where possible.

| **Process** | **Self-Serve Tool** | **Human Touch Point** |
|---|---|---|
| Onboarding compliance | Digital checklist (Notion/Airtable) | Day 1 welcome call |
| Platform training | Recorded video tutorials + knowledge base | Live Q&A session |
| Report template guidance | Template with inline help text + examples | Peer review for first 5 reports |
| Building code reference | Searchable regulation database (Notion) | Monthly "Regulation Roundup" |
| Quality feedback | Automated audit scorecard | 1:1 feedback for scores <85% |
| Career progression | Transparent level criteria document | Quarterly career conversations |
| Client communication | Email/WhatsApp templates | Escalation only for issues |
| Payment (contractors) | Automated invoice processing | Exception handling only |
