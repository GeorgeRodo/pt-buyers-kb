## THE PROBLEM

Why August 2026 Changes Everything

---

### The State Steps Back

DL 108/2026 eliminates municipal pre-verification of construction. The *comunicacao previa* regime means properties have "paper approval" without physical confirmation. The duty to verify is now private.

### The Court Steps Up

The Tribunal da Relacao de Lisboa (Processo 325/21.1T8MLD.P1) ruled that document-only verification is a "tautologia." The law demands physical investigation -- "averiguacao para apuramento da verdade."

### You Are Left Exposed

The first defect surfaces in Year 2. The seller has walked away. The builder's company is dissolved. You -- with EUR 150,000 insurance and 20-year prescription -- are the only insured, traceable target. And with that insurance policy, you are the easy target.
