### Detailed Model Profiles

#### Model 1: Real Estate Agent Referral Network

**How it works:** Estate agents refer buyers to inspection firms, typically for a referral fee (5-15%) or as a value-add service. In mature markets (NL, UK), this is formalized. In PT/ES, it is entirely informal and opaque.

**Examples by Market:**

| Market | Example | Mechanism | Economics |
|--------|---------|-----------|-----------|
| **Netherlands** | BIJN.nl + NVM makelaars | Formal referral partnerships with ~70% market share agents | Commission/referral fee |
| **Netherlands** | Perfectkeur makelaars page | Dedicated makelaar landing page with co-marketing | Co-marketing + referral fees |
| **Netherlands** | Woningkeur | Local makelaar relationships through franchise network | Regional partnerships |
| **UK** | reallymoving / Compare My Move | Agent-adjacent; comparison at point of purchase | Lead fees |
| **Portugal** | Brint Portugal | Buyer's agent coordinates third-party inspections | €400-800 coordination fee |
| **Portugal** | RE/MAX Cidadela, IAD Portugal | Educational content + inspector referrals | Informal referral |
| **Spain** | Housfy | Flat-fee agency; potential inspection distribution partner | Not yet active |
| **Spain** | Tecnocasa/Redpiso/Don Piso | Traditional franchise networks; local inspector referrals | Informal |
| **Germany** | Black Label Immobilien | Brokerage + valuation for expats; no technical inspection | N/A |

**InspectOS Opportunity:** In PT and ES, where agent referral is entirely informal, InspectOS can create the first formalized "Certified Agent Program" with co-marketing materials, referral fees, and shared branding.

---

#### Model 2: Lender Panel Integration

**How it works:** Mortgage lenders maintain approved panels of surveyors/inspectors. Lender-instructed valuations/surveys are high-volume, lower-margin work that provides steady revenue.

**Examples by Market:**

| Market | Example | Scale | Economics |
|--------|---------|-------|-----------|
| **UK** | e.surv / LSL Property Services | ~38% market share of surveyor-led valuations; 500 surveyors; 6 of 7 largest UK lenders | High volume, lower margin |
| **UK** | Countrywide Surveying Services | 700+ staff, 450+ RICS surveyors; tablet-based reporting | Panel management fees |
| **UK** | Connells Survey & Valuation | 500+ team; serves 40+ lenders; ISO 9001 | Lender panel fees |
| **UK** | Legal & General / Meridian | £43.7M revenue (2024); major banks/building societies | Insurance-aligned panel |
| **Netherlands** | NHG compliance providers | Inspection required when achterstallig onderhoud ≥10% of property value | Standard inspection fees |
| **Germany** | Bank appraisals (Immobilienbewertung) | GRUNDWERT, ImWert, Dr. Mechkat — court-certified for banks | €1,500-5,000+ per appraisal |

**InspectOS Opportunity:** Portugal and Spain have NO lender-mandated inspection relationships. This is a massive untapped channel. InspectOS should target Portuguese mortgage banks (Millennium bcp, Novo Banco, Santander Totta) and Spanish lenders to create "inspection-required" mortgage products.

## 2. Partnership Architecture Map by Country

### 2.1 Portugal — B2B Greenfield (Virtually No Competition)

**B2B Maturity: N/A — Greenfield Opportunity**

Portugal represents the most attractive B2B greenfield opportunity in Europe. No competitor has built ANY formal B2B channel. The entire market operates through informal word-of-mouth and agent referrals.

```
PORTUGAL B2B ARCHITECTURE (Current State)
==========================================

[Buyer] ←───informal referral───→ [Solo Engineer/Architect]
    ↑                                  (60-70% of inspections)
    │                                      │
    └─────expat networks──────────────┘    │
                                          │
[Real Estate Agent] ──opaque referral───┘
    (no formal partnerships, no fees)

INSTITUTIONAL LAYER:
  ADENE ──EPC certification (mandatory)
  DGEG ──Gas inspection certification (mandatory)
  ISQ SAVE ──B2B/corporate diagnostics (no consumer channel)

NO lender integrations
NO insurance partnerships (consumer-facing)
NO proptech integrations
NO comparison platforms
NO franchise networks
NO API ecosystem
```

**B2B Gap Analysis for Portugal:**

| B2B Channel | Exists? | Leader | Gap Size |
|-------------|---------|--------|----------|
| Agent referral networks | No (informal only) | N/A | Massive |
| Lender-mandated inspection | No | N/A | Massive |
| Insurance partnerships | No (ISQ SAVE B2B only) | N/A | Massive |
| White-label | No | N/A | Massive |
| API ecosystem | No | N/A | Massive |
| Franchise | No | N/A | Massive |
| Comparison platform | No | N/A | Massive |
| PropTech integration | Partial (PropCheck) | InspectOS | Large |
| Regulatory compliance | Yes (ADENE, DGEG) | Government | N/A |

**InspectOS Competitive Advantage in Portugal:**
- Only player with PropCheck integration
- Only player positioning around regulatory urgency (DL 10/2024, DL 108/2026)
- "Simplex Safe" product is unique regulatory-first offering
- Bilingual EN/PT reports = competitive moat for expat segment
- 48h guaranteed delivery unmatched by traditional engineers
