# ISQ (SAVE / SAVE VALOR)

Portugal's largest inspection and certification body — hundreds of engineers nationwide, established relationships with insurers, banks, and public entities. Runs **SAVE** (*Serviço de Avaliação e Verificação de Edifícios*), a building-diagnostics service, and **SAVE VALOR**, a product built specifically for insurance companies. Distinct from [[adene|ADENE]] (energy certification) and [[registo-predial|Registo Predial]] (land registry) — ISQ is a private inspection/certification competitor, not a public registry.

## Why it matters to InspectOS

- **Talent competitor**: ISQ's scale and institutional client base make it a formidable competitor for senior inspection talent — see [[hiring-talent-market]].
- **B2B competitor, but with a gap**: ISQ SAVE is B2B-only with no consumer channel — it inspects for insurers, not for individual buyers. Per [[inspectos-b2b-ecosystem]] and [[src-b2b-ecosystem-analysis]], this leaves the entire lender-panel and consumer-facing inspection channel open in Portugal, unlike the UK/Netherlands where lender-mandated inspection is mature. Named alongside [[entities-hub|PIDS]] (Portugal's top *consumer*-facing inspection competitor) as one of the two incumbents neither of which has closed the B2B gap.
- **Accreditation scope gap**: per [[inspectos-iso17020-accreditation]], ISQ holds ISO 17020 accreditation for other scopes (energy, industrial) but not for residential PCA — a specific accreditation gap InspectOS's own 17020 pathway is designed to exploit.
