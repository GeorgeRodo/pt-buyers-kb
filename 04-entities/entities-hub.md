# Entities hub

Institutions, portals, and registries the venture interacts with. One-liners here; promote to a full page (like [[adene]]) when an entity is referenced across 3+ pages or has operational nuance worth capturing.

## Registries & verification
- [[registo-predial|**Registo Predial**]] — certidão predial (title, encumbrances, ownership chain); area-discrepancy cross-check vs. caderneta. Full page.
- **Portal das Finanças / Casa Pronta** — caderneta predial; registered transaction prices for [[comparables]].
- [[adene|**ADENE**]] — energy-certificate registry; EPC fraud verification. Full page.
- [[alojamento-local|**RNAAL / Alojamento Local**]] — national AL registry; licence validity and containment-zone checks. Full page.

## Regulators & authorities
- **IMPIC** — AMI licensing for mediation; the agency's regulator.
- **ASAE** — enforcement incl. AL fire-safety complaints (cross-reference channel for [[scie-fire-safety|SCIE]] findings).
- **Câmaras Municipais** — licensing, PDM geoportals, urbanistic DD; post-[[dl-10-2024-simplex|Simplex]], no longer inspecting builds.
- **Banco de Portugal (BdP)** — mortgage macro rules, statistics.

## Technical & scientific
- **LNEC** — national civil-engineering lab; seismic microzonation maps (Lisbon), pathology literature.
- **LNEG** — geological maps: expansive soils, salinity aggressiveness.
- **RCAAP** — Portuguese open-access research repository; the prompt chain's citation backbone.
- **Ordem dos Engenheiros (OE) / Ordem dos Arquitectos (OA)** — professional orders; source of *Peritos* for NDT and court expertise.

## Market data & tools
- **INE** — official statistics; monthly transaction data.
- **Idealista / Imovirtual / Casa Sapo** — portals (Pro tiers for agents).
- CRM (HubSpot/Pipedrive/Notion) · DocuSign · Sumsub/Onfido (KYC) · DeepL/sworn translators — the [[phase-1-onboarding|Phase 1]] tool stack.
