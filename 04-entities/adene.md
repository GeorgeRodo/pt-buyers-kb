# ADENE

The national energy agency; operates the energy-certificate (EPC / *certificado energético*) registry.

## Operational use

- **Desktop audit step** ([[phase-4-due-diligence|P4 phase 1]]): verify the listing's claimed EPC against the ADENE registry before the visit — mismatch or missing certificate is an immediate flag.
- **EPC fraud field check:** measure visible insulation thickness at window reveals; **<4cm visible where the EPC claims 6–8cm = fraudulent certificate** — a Tier-2 price-slashing item ([[phase-6-offer-cpcv|P6]]) because post-build insulation retrofit is disproportionately expensive.
- **Content angle:** "Why your new-build EPC rating is probably a lie (how to check ADENE)" — Pillar 1, [[content-strategy]].
