# Alojamento Local (AL) — registry, licensing & containment zones

*Alojamento Local* (AL, short-term/tourist rental) registration under **RNAAL**, the national AL registry, is required for any residential rental of 30 days or less to non-residents. Promoted from a one-liner in [[entities-hub]] on 2026-07-14 — cited across 8+ pages (regional dossiers, DD streams, post-purchase, market intelligence) with no dedicated page, past the atomicity "promote at 3+" threshold.

## The post-Mais Habitação regime (2026)

The *Mais Habitação* package significantly altered AL licensing. As of 2026:

- New AL licences are **suspended in designated containment zones** (*áreas de contenção*) — primarily central Lisbon (Baixa, Chiado, Alfama, Bairro Alto, Príncipe Real) and central Porto (Ribeira, Aliados, Cedofeita) — unless the property already held a licence or qualifies for a specific exemption. See [[lisbon]] and [[porto]] for region-specific detail.
- Existing licences face **periodic review, renewed every 5 years**; non-renewal is possible in containment zones where the Câmara determines "pressure on housing" is excessive.
- The licence must be displayed on the property; guest ID collection and reporting to SEF/AIMA is mandatory; noise, waste, and behavioural rules carry penalties.
- AL income is taxed under specific IRS rules; non-resident owners face withholding tax and cannot deduct expenses the way resident owners can.

> [!warning] AL licences **do not automatically transfer** to a new owner on sale — the buyer must re-register, typically straightforward but not instant. A property advertised as "AL-ready" or "with AL potential" inside a containment zone may in fact be unlicensable; treat any such claim with suspicion until RNAAL-verified.

## Why this matters across the wiki

- **[[phase-4-due-diligence|Phase 4]]**: if the buyer's investment case depends on AL income, licence validity must be verified in DD — never assumed from the listing. RNAAL extract is a mandatory DD document (see [[agency-checklists|Appendix B.3]]).
- **[[cpcv|CPCV]]**: the AL Licence Condition Precedent clause (Appendix C.3.10) makes the contract voidable, or opens price renegotiation, if RNAAL verification fails after signing.
- **[[phase-8-post-purchase|Phase 8]]**: AL is one of six property-management models compared in the playbook (owner self-managed, long-term managed/self-managed, AL managed/self-managed, hybrid) — AL-managed offers higher but volatile yield (€50–150/night typical) against 15–25% manager fees and licensing risk; AL self-managed adds significant time cost against 14–20% platform fees.
- **Regional dossiers**: [[lisbon]] and [[porto]] both carry containment-zone detail; [[algarve]] treats AL economics as a market driver rather than a containment risk since Algarve zones are less restricted.
