# IMPIC — Instituto dos Mercados Públicos, do Imobiliário e da Construção

The regulator behind **AMI licensing** (*Autorização para Mediação Imobiliária*) — the mandatory license every real estate mediator/agent and, by extension, [[platform-buyers-agency|buyer's agency]] operation must hold to legally intermediate property transactions in Portugal. Any buyer's-agency BRA or listing agreement should carry a verifiable AMI/IMPIC registration number — see [[client-onboarding-toolkit]]'s intake fields.

## Where it shows up in the wiki

- **Compliance baseline** for [[platform-buyers-agency]]: AMI licensing via IMPIC sits alongside GDPR and AML/KYC as the non-negotiable regulatory floor.
- **Regulatory-change monitoring**: [[market-intelligence]] tracks IMPIC (alongside BdP, AT, Câmaras) on a continuous cadence as a source of rule changes that affect the agency business.
- **DL 108/2026 enforcement**: one of the InspectOS strategy sources cites a flat €25,000 IMPIC fine threshold for the new law — this figure conflicts with the legal-impact-assessment sources' solidary-liability fines up to €450,000; tracked as open item #3 in [[open-decisions-tracker]], not resolved here.
- **Standard-setting leverage** (aspirational, per [[inspectos-iso17020-accreditation]]): IMPIC is named as a regulator that could eventually mandate "a certified PCA" in regulation without naming InspectOS directly — a potential moat if InspectOS's methodology becomes the de facto reference standard. This is strategy-corpus speculation, not a current fact.
