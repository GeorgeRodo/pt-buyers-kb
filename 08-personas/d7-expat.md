# Persona — D7 expat

A non-EU buyer pursuing Portugal's D7 (passive income) visa, for whom the property purchase is entangled with the residency application rather than a standalone transaction. [[phase-1-onboarding|Phase 1]] carries extra weight: NIF, fiscal representative, and visa-lawyer coordination happen in parallel with buyer qualification, not after it. [[phase-5-financing|Phase 5]] is complicated by non-EUR income proof requirements the visa process demands independently of the mortgage. Timeline pressure is real but self-imposed by visa-application deadlines rather than market conditions, which makes this persona unusually vulnerable to the "manufactured urgency" red flags flagged elsewhere in the wiki.
