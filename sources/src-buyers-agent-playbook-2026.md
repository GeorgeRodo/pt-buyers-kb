## What it is
A complete operating manual for buyer-only representation in Portugal: 11 chapters + 3 appendices. Chapters 2–9 define the eight-phase buyer journey that serves as this wiki's spine; Ch. 10 profiles seven property types; Ch. 11 covers cross-cutting topics (ethics, regions, expat risk, compliance, fee models, 2026 trends); appendices hold the BRA template, master checklists, scripts and clause language.

## Key claims extracted
- Structural seller bias: DL 118/2013 aligns the *mediador* with the seller; buyer-only fiduciary representation is the counter-position.
- Quantified pipeline norms: 8–20 candidates in 4–6 weeks; 15–30% off-market discovery; five-layer sourcing stack with market-share estimates.
- Economics: ~1.5% fees; €60–100k+ 5-year client value; referrals 50–70% of mature mandates.
- 2026 regulatory snapshot: AL containment zones, NHR closure, young-buyer mortgage guarantee — all flagged for annual re-verification.
