# Source — InspectOS Talent Master Plan

**Raw files:** `raw/01_Talent_Intelligence.md` · `02_Sourcing_Architecture.md` · `03_Quality_Gates.md` · `04_Compensation_Contracts.md` · `05_Onboarding_Retention.md` · `06_90Day_Roadmap.md` · `07_Strategic_Ecosystem.md` · `08_Non_Inspector_Hiring_Plan.md` · `10_Job_Vacancies (1).md` + `10_Ofertas_de_Emprego_PT.md` (EN/PT pair, same three postings) · **Ingested:** 2026-07-20.

## What it is

An 8-part internal hiring and operations playbook for staffing InspectOS's inspector network and the non-inspector functions around it (legal, ops, sales, tech, finance), plus three ready-to-post job vacancies. Each part cross-references the others ("Prompts 1–5", "the original Prompt 2") — this is one continuous planning document, not eight independent sources, so it gets one source page, mirroring how the 12-module construction-defects prompt chain is filed as a single source.

This is company-internal HR/operations material, not buyer-journey or platform-technical content — it doesn't extend any existing phase or domain page. Per Paul's direction, it's filed as a new `05-strategy/` cluster under a dedicated hiring hub rather than forced into an existing folder.

## Part-by-part summary

1. **Talent intelligence** ([[hiring-talent-market]]) — Portugal's OE-registered engineer supply (64,261 members, 30,499 civil), structural shortage signals, the Brazilian CREA-to-OE reciprocity pathway, Angolan contractor-support pathway, compensation benchmarks, competitive landscape (ISQ, Teixeira Duarte, Mota-Engil, energy utilities).
2. **Sourcing architecture** ([[hiring-sourcing-channels]]) — 7-channel sourcing system (OE Direct, university pipeline, Brazilian funnel, Angolan network, job boards, agencies, referrals) with a 90-day activation sequence and ~€8,500–€12,000 budget.
3. **Quality gates** ([[hiring-quality-gates]]) — 7-gate vetting pipeline from OE verification through a standardized trial inspection, built around the legal fact that only OE-registered engineers (or OA-registered architects) can sign court-admissible inspection reports.
4. **Compensation & contracts** ([[hiring-compensation]]) — four inspector profiles (FT senior, contractor, Brazilian transfer, intern), full employer-cost breakdowns, and the freelancer tax-optimization math that makes the contractor model attractive to both sides.
5. **Onboarding & retention** ([[hiring-onboarding-retention]]) — 90-day onboarding journey, 5-module training curriculum, tiered random-audit QA loop, and a documented career ladder (Inspector → Senior → Team Lead → Regional Manager) as the primary retention lever.
6. **90-day recruitment roadmap** ([[hiring-90day-roadmap]]) — the execution plan translating parts 1–5 into a phased €11,500 budget, targeting 5 vetted contractors + 2 full-time seniors by Day 90, with a named risk register.
7. **Strategic ecosystem** ([[hiring-strategic-moats]]) — the 12–24 month plan to convert hiring from a transactional function into a moat: formal OE partnership, "InspectOS Academy" university brand, "Portuguese Market Entry Program" for Brazilian engineers, an inspector certification mark, and B2B talent licensing.
8. **Non-inspector hiring plan** ([[hiring-non-inspector-roles]]) — legal/compliance, operations, sales/marketing, technology, and finance hiring sequenced across Year 1 (~€140k–€192k), built on a heavy-outsourcing-first philosophy.
9. **Job vacancies** ([[hiring-job-vacancies]]) — three ready-to-post listings (Senior Property Inspector, Operations & Compliance Manager, External Legal Counsel) that operationalize parts 3, 4, and 8.
