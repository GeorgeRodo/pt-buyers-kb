# Job vacancies — three ready-to-post listings

Three postings (dated June 2026, EN and PT versions) that operationalize [[hiring-quality-gates]], [[hiring-compensation]], and [[hiring-non-inspector-roles]] into copy-ready job descriptions. Kept as a wiki page rather than left only in `raw/` because the compensation figures and requirement lists here are the canonical, publish-ready version — future postings should be diffed against this baseline rather than redrafted from scratch.

## Position 1 — Senior Property Inspector (Lisbon/Porto, FT or contractor)

Requires active OE registration with signing authority, an active Ageas professional-liability declaration (or willingness to obtain one immediately), 3+ years of relevant experience, a Portuguese driver's license and own vehicle, and Portuguese fluency (reports must be court-admissible). Desirable: ADENE assessor qualification, diagnostic-equipment experience, English fluency. Full-time package: €38,000–€50,000 base, ~€58,000–€72,000 total employer investment. Contractor package: €250–€350/inspection with optional €500 or €1,000/month retainer tiers. Explicitly welcomes Brazilian CREA-registered applicants and career-transition candidates from construction site management.

## Position 2 — Operations & Compliance Manager (Lisbon, FT)

The foundational operations hire described in [[hiring-non-inspector-roles]] — inspector-network management (35%), scheduling/dispatch (25%), quality assurance and compliance including OE/insurance monthly verification and acting as internal DPO liaison (25%), training and process ownership (10%), reporting (5%). Requires 4+ years operations experience managing distributed/field teams, Portuguese and English fluency, and a driver's license. €35,000–€45,000 base, €47,000–€61,000 total employer investment. Explicit 6-month success criteria: network grown from 10 to 15+ with zero compliance failures, sub-36-hour average report delivery, ≥85% quality audit average, ≥4.2/5.0 client satisfaction.

## Position 3 — External Legal Counsel (Lisbon/remote, retainer)

A strategic advisory engagement, not litigation — contracts and commercial (40%: employment, contractor, client T&Cs, partnership MOUs), regulatory and compliance (30%: DL 10/2024 monitoring, OE/IMPIC advice, disclaimer defensibility, GDPR coordination with the DPO), risk and liability (20%), corporate (10%: fundraising docs, international-expansion structure). Requires Ordem dos Advogados registration, 5+ years post-qualification experience, and specialization in at least two of real estate law, construction law, engineering professional liability, or commercial contracts. €2,500–€4,000/month retainer covering 15–25 hours of advisory work, €150–€250/hour for matters outside scope (litigation, complex fundraising).
