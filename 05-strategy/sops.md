# SOPs — internal training manual

The 12-module knowledge base rendered as operating procedure. SOPs are executions of canonical wiki pages — update the page, regenerate the SOP.

- **SOP 1 — Desktop audit** (modules 1–2): ADENE, Registo Predial, RCAAP/licensing checks before any site visit. Canonical: [[phase-4-due-diligence|P4 step 1]], [[adene]].
- **SOP 2 — Exterior scan** (modules 4, 5, 8): 0.3mm crack threshold, X-crack escalation, ETICS drip-edge, geological red flags by zip code. Canonical: P4 step 2, [[regions-hub]].
- **SOP 3 — Interior flip-detection** (module 3): mandatory hammer sounding, moisture mapping behind furniture, under-sink PB check. Canonical: [[instagram-renovation-detection]].
- **SOP 4 — Report writing**: never "faulty window"; always mechanism + implication + regulatory hook ("air leakage at frame periphery indicating absent thermal break; verify against declared EPC parameters"). The report language IS the legal weapon ([[dl-67-2023]]) and the premium-pricing justification.
