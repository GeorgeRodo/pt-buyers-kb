# Sourcing channel architecture

Seven channels, each mapped to a target profile, expected volume, and cost per hire. Three are "fast win" channels capable of producing candidates within 14 days: **OE Direct** (the Bolsa de Emprego job board — 64,261-member audience, free posting, 3–7 days to first candidate), **freelance/contractor direct outreach** to individual OE-registered sole practitioners, and **job boards + careers page** (Indeed.pt, Net-Empregos, SAPO Emprego, LinkedIn — 2–7 days to first application).

## The seven channels

1. **OE Direct** — the single highest-precision channel: 100% OE-registered audience, free, moderated within 24–48 hours. Rated Tier 1 priority.
2. **University pipeline** — FEUP (Porto, 8,000+ students), IST (Lisbon, most prestigious, Técnico Job Bank with 2,000+ registered employers), University of Coimbra (Centro coverage), University of Minho (Braga/Norte). Feeds both immediate junior hiring and the IEFP-subsidized internship system (+Talento program: government reimburses 65–80% of a €1,057/month Master's stipend, netting InspectOS €211–€370/month per intern).
3. **Brazilian CREA-to-OE funnel** — two tiers: Brazilians already in Portugal (LinkedIn, Facebook community groups, CREA state-chapter partnerships) and Brazilians in Brazil considering relocation (Vagas.com, Indeed Brasil, CONFEA dissemination). Candidates are pre-qualified against the reciprocity criteria (see [[hiring-talent-market]]) before entering the vetting pipeline.
4. **Angolan practical talent** — not a registration pathway, a contractor-support pool: Teixeira Duarte Angola rotation, Mota-Engil Angola operations, Angolan diaspora networks in Lisbon.
5. **Digital & job board mix** — Net-Empregos, Indeed.pt, SAPO Emprego, LinkedIn Jobs ranked Tier 1; Expresso Emprego, Alerta Emprego, EnglishJobs.pt Tier 2. Staged spend: €0 in weeks 1–2 (free listings), rising to €300–€800/month by week 5+ as paid promotion is added.
6. **Agency & partner layer** — Michael Page, Hays, Randstad, Adecco at 15–20% of first-year salary contingency fees; used sparingly (1–2 agencies, no exclusivity) given InspectOS's small hiring volume.
7. **Referral & ecosystem engine** — tiered referral bonuses (€250 for a junior/intern hire up to €1,000 for a senior full-time hire after 6 months) plus partnership referrals from real estate agencies, builder-warranty firms, engineering consultancies, and mortgage brokers.

## 90-day activation sequence

OE Direct and job boards go live Week 1; university outreach Week 2; LinkedIn promotion and the referral program Week 3; the Brazilian channel Week 4; agency engagement Weeks 5–6; IEFP internship postings Weeks 7–8; ecosystem partnerships Weeks 9–10; optimization and reallocation to the top-3 performing channels Weeks 11–12. Total 90-day sourcing budget: €8,500–€12,000.
