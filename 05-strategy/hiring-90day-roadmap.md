# 90-day recruitment execution roadmap

The plan that ties [[hiring-talent-market]], [[hiring-sourcing-channels]], [[hiring-quality-gates]], and [[hiring-compensation]] together into a phased, budgeted execution sequence targeting **5 vetted contractors and 2 full-time senior inspectors by Day 90**, covering both Lisbon and Porto.

## The three phases

**Phase 1 — Foundation (Days 1–30, €2,800):** before a single job is posted, InspectOS needs finalized job descriptions, a live careers page, OE employer registration, contract templates reviewed by a labor lawyer, an insurance-verification SOP, and 2–3 trial-inspection properties secured. **Phase 2 — Activation (Days 31–60, €4,200):** all seven sourcing channels go live simultaneously (36–69 expected applications), screening and vetting cycles run (application review → phone screen → structured interview → trial inspection), and the contractor pool builds toward 5 vetted. **Phase 3 — Conversion (Days 61–90, €4,500):** first full-time offers extended, contractor agreements signed and active, the first IEFP intern cohort onboarded, and quality-metric baselines established (≥85% average report quality, ≥95% on-time delivery, ≥4.2/5.0 client satisfaction).

Total 90-day recruitment budget: **€11,500**, blending to roughly €910–€1,250 cost-per-hire across profiles once salary/stipend pass-through costs are excluded.

## Risk register

The three risks judged most likely to derail the timeline: (1) an insufficient pool of OE-registered applicants — mitigated by leading with the Brazilian CREA-to-OE channel and activating the university pipeline early; (2) a Brazilian candidate's OEP admission running past 90 days — mitigated by the interim-trainee structure, which keeps the candidate engaged and paid without letting them sign reports prematurely; (3) trial-inspection failure rates exceeding 50% — mitigated by calibrating the trial properties correctly and using the conditional-pass band (65–79%) rather than a hard pass/fail cliff. Phase-gate go/no-go criteria are explicit at each 30-day checkpoint, with defined fallbacks (extend the phase, increase ad spend, engage a recruitment agency) rather than open-ended slippage.
