# InspectOS B2B ecosystem strategy — Portugal first-mover land grab

Distinct from [[b2b-partnerships]] (which covers the buyer's-agency platform's lawyer/agency channel) — this is the inspection platform's B2B distribution strategy, and its central finding is stark: **Portugal currently has zero formal B2B inspection partnerships of any kind**, across a 15-model taxonomy benchmarked against Spain, the Netherlands, the UK, and Germany. Every channel category — agent referral networks, lender panels, insurance bundling, PropTech portal integration, professional-association distribution — is either entirely informal or completely absent in Portugal today.

## The named competitive gap

**PIDS** is named as Portugal's top inspection competitor, with no B2B partnerships at all. ISQ SAVE VALOR (already tracked as a competitor in [[hiring-talent-market]]) is B2B-only with no consumer channel. Neither has closed any of the gaps below — the window is explicitly described as open.

## Five channel opportunities, ranked

1. **Lender partnerships (P0).** No mortgage lender in Portugal or Spain maintains an approved inspection panel — a "massive untapped channel" by the source's own framing, versus very mature lender-panel integration in the UK and Netherlands. Named targets: Millennium bcp, Novo Banco, Santander Totta, for an "inspection-required mortgage" product.
2. **PropTech portal integration (P0).** Called the single biggest untapped B2B opportunity in Europe — no inspection company anywhere has a "Book Inspection" button embedded in a major listing portal. Imovirtual (3M+ monthly visits), CasaSAPO, and Supercasa are the Portugal-specific targets.
3. **Insurance partnership (P1).** Fidelidade (Portugal's largest insurer) is the named target for an "inspected property discount" product — modeled on the Netherlands' Woongarant + Perfectkeur bundle, which prices inspection at €449 through the insurance channel there.
4. **Certified agent program (P0, Q3 2026).** Formalizing what's currently informal referral relationships (per [[hiring-talent-market]]'s existing agent-referral notes) into a structured "InspectOS Certified Agent" program: 10% referral fee, co-marketing materials, shared branding, agent training.
5. **Regulatory-channel deepening.** ADENE and DGEG relationships already exist informally around EPC and gas-certificate work; the recommendation is to formalize InspectOS as the recommended bundling provider for both.

## The untapped category: B2C subscription

No B2C subscription inspection model exists anywhere in the European market surveyed — a "Property Health Subscription" (annual inspection + compliance tracking + maintenance reminders) is flagged as completely open. This would sit alongside the per-inspection PCA pricing discussed in [[inspectos-pca-pcs-strategy]], not replace it.

## Priority and resourcing

The five critical moat-building actions for the next 12 months, in order: secure an idealista integration (Spain, transformative but not Portugal-specific), launch the Certified Agent program (Portugal, target 50+ agent partners in 6 months), build and launch an InspectOS API v1 (cross-border), close an insurance partnership with Fidelidade or Allianz Portugal, and develop an AI-powered digital pre-inspection tool (aimed at the Netherlands market). Recommended B2B investment split: 35% Portugal, 30% Spain, 15% Netherlands, 10% Germany, 5% UK, 5% cross-border API platform — reflecting that Portugal is the defendable home-market play while Spain is the largest single expansion opportunity.

International-market detail (Spain, Netherlands, UK, Germany channel maturity, named competitors, specific partnership targets) is preserved in [[src-b2b-ecosystem-analysis]] and `raw/b2b_ecosystem_analysis.md` but not exhaustively reproduced here — this KB's scope is Portugal buyer-side; non-Portugal detail is reference material for a future international-expansion phase, not active strategy.
