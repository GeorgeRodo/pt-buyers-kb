# Social media playbook — platform-native posting rules

[[content-strategy]] defines *what* to say (four pillars) and roughly *where* (short-form vs. long-form). This page defines *how* to post it on each platform so Exgest drafts don't need re-deriving these rules every time. Rules below are current external best practice (web research, July 2026), not internal data — treat as a starting default, not sacred numbers, and revisit as platforms change algorithms.

> [!warning] Externally sourced, unverified against our own account performance. No `sources/` page backs this — there is no first-party data yet (no posting history to benchmark against). Re-check when we have our own engagement data.

## Instagram (Reels)
Serves Pillar 1 (Scam Exposer) and Pillar 4 (Tool Masterclass) — the field-test and visual-proof formats.

- **Length:** 30–90s for standard engagement; 7–15s if deliberately chasing trend/reach.
- **Hook:** first 1–3s must show the payoff (cracked wall, moisture meter spike) — no slow intros.
- **Cadence:** 3–5 Reels/week, consistently, beats bursty posting.
- **Hashtags:** 2–3 highly relevant only; hashtags now act as topic labels, not a discovery lever. Mix one niche tag (<500K posts) with one broader one.
- **Carousels:** primary format for Pillar 4 (tool masterclasses) — higher save rate than Reels for reference-style content, and saves are a strong algorithm signal.

## TikTok
Serves Pillar 1 (Scam Exposer) primarily; same field-test clips can be cut for both platforms.

- **Length:** 11–18s for viral/trend attempts, 21–34s for a mini-story (defect reveal → explanation), up to 60s for anything educational (era autopsy teaser).
- **Hook:** 3-second rule — if the viewer isn't hooked by second 3, the algorithm won't push it. Structure every clip as hook → middle → CTA.
- **Completion rate is the dominant ranking signal** (70%+ target) — pacing and payoff timing matter more than production polish.
- **Cadence:** 3–5/week sweet spot; daily posting is a reasonable ramp-up strategy for a new account.
- **First-60-minutes engagement is weighted heavily** — don't post and disappear; a founder/team reply-and-engage window right after posting matters.

## YouTube (Shorts + long-form)
Long-form serves Pillar 2 (Legal Timebomb) and Pillar 3 (Era-Specific Autopsy) — the trust-building, high-consideration content that actually converts HNW/B2B leads. Shorts serve discovery and feed off long-form.

- **Split:** early-stage channel, lean 70% Shorts / 30% long-form for growth; shift toward 50/50 once established. As a floor, one long-form video per 4–6 short-form pieces.
- **Shorts are a growth and top-of-funnel tool, not a closer** — for a high-consideration service like inspection or buyer's agency, long-form is what actually earns enough trust for someone to book.
- **Shorts sourcing:** don't create Shorts from scratch — pull the strongest 60s from each long-form Pillar 2/3 video and post it as a Short linking back to the full video.
- **Cadence:** 1–2 Shorts/week is a workable floor for a real-estate-adjacent channel; long-form cadence is dictated by production capacity, not the algorithm.

## LinkedIn
Serves Pillar 2 (Legal Timebomb) for the B2B / premium-agency / lawyer-partnership audience — see [[b2b-partnerships]].

- **Post length:** 1,300–2,000 characters (roughly 200–300 words) of conversational, line-broken text outperforms short posts; mobile-first formatting (short line breaks) is mandatory since >60% of traffic is mobile.
- **Engagement hierarchy:** saves > long comments (9+ words boost the post 3x) > likes. Write for saves — reference-style, "the rule that will cost you your lawsuit" framing works because people bookmark it.
- **Links:** 3+ relevant links in the post body reportedly boosts reach; don't be shy about linking to the full legal-timebomb piece or a lead magnet.
- **Hashtags:** cap at 3 — more than that measurably hurts reach.
- **Cadence:** 2–3/week minimum viable, 4–5/week optimal, weekdays, Tue–Thu 8–10am target-audience local time.
