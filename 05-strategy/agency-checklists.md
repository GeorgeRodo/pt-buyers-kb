# Agency checklists — operational reference

Printable, on-site operating checklists for the [[platform-buyers-agency|buyer's agency platform]], consolidated from playbook Appendix B. These are the agency's counterpart to [[sops|the inspection platform's SOPs]]: canonical phase pages hold the reasoning, these checklists are the field-usable execution layer. Customise per engagement; regenerate from source rather than hand-edit.

## B.1 — General on-site viewing checklist

Universal layer applied to every viewing: approach (parking, noise, neighbours), building exterior (facade, cracks, roof), common areas (lift age/service, security, CCTV), living areas (floor/wall/ceiling condition, light, noise insulation), windows/doors, kitchen, bathrooms, utilities (electric panel age/RCD, water meter, heating), basement/storage, outdoor space, parking, condominium noticeboard (pending works, arrears notices), general condition (smell, pests), and listing-agent pressure signals. Canonical: [[phase-3-viewings]].

## B.2 — Property-type-specific viewing additions

Layered on top of B.1 per type — full detail in each type's page under [[property-types-hub]]:

- Historical: crack mapping, timber beam/floor deflection, roof structure, damp course, heritage plaque, prior renovation approvals, seismic retrofit history. See [[property-type-historical]].
- New build/off-plan: showcase-unit quality, snagging process, parking/storage allocation, handover protocol, *seguro decenal*, EPC, penalty clauses, DL 156/2005 bank guarantee. See [[property-type-new-build-off-plan]].
- Land/development: topography, access road, utilities at boundary, cadastral markers, flooding/erosion evidence, PDM zoning, urbanistic parameters, environmental constraints. See [[property-type-land-development]].
- Luxury: security system, smart-home infrastructure, staff quarters, guest parking, concierge service level, view-protection planning risk, privacy/screening. See [[property-type-luxury]].
- Rural/quinta: water source and rights documentation, access road quality, septic system, boundaries, agricultural buildings, forestry compliance, habitational licence/rebuild potential. See [[property-type-rural-quinta]].

## B.3 — Due diligence document checklist

Comprehensive DD document set (lawyer chases, agent coordinates): *caderneta predial* (urbana/rústica), *certidão predial permanente* (issued within 30 days of CPCV), *licença de utilização*, *alvará de licença de construção*, *termo de responsabilidade* and *ficha técnica de habitação* (post-2004 new builds), EPC, condominium minutes/reserve fund/debt certificate/regulation/insurance, licensed floor plans, 12 months of utility bills, seller ID and marital/spousal-consent documents, corporate documents for a corporate seller, tenant documentation if let, AL/RNAAL licence, structural and energy reports if commissioned, topographic/geotechnical reports for land/rural, heritage classification (IGESPAR/DGPC), PDM zoning extract, environmental and forestry compliance confirmations. Canonical: [[phase-4-due-diligence]].

## B.4 — Pre-closing verification checklist

Run 48 hours before deed, every item confirmed before proceeding: title (current *certidão predial*, mortgage cancellation letter), licences (*licença de utilização*, EPC, *ficha técnica*, construction licence for new builds), condominium (debt certificate, reserve fund, regulation), taxes (IMT/IS paid via DUIC, seller's IMI cleared, capital gains confirmed), mortgage (formal offer with notary, funds transferred), funds (balance transferred, source-of-funds documentation), documents (*caderneta*, floor plans, CPCV + addenda, inventory, POA if applicable), identity (passports, 2 witnesses), logistics (notary slot, translator, keys inventory), insurance (multi-risk and mortgage life insurance bound). Canonical: [[phase-7-closing]].

## B.5 — Handover checklist

Keys (all entrances, garage, mailbox, storage), remotes and access codes, documents (certified *escritura* copy, EPC, *ficha técnica*, warranties, manuals, condo regulation, recent bills, insurance certificates), meter readings (photographed), condition walk-through with damage documentation, utility transfers initiated, condominium administrator notified, insurance active, seller mail-forwarding arranged (60 days recommended). Canonical: [[phase-8-post-purchase]].

## B.6 — Snagging checklist (new build/off-plan)

Run within 5 days of handover, ideally with an independent snagging surveyor; defects documented in writing and sent to the developer by registered letter within 30 days (arms the [[dl-67-2023|DL 67/2023]] clock). Covers doors, windows, walls, ceilings, floors, kitchen, bathrooms, electrical (including RCD/earth test), plumbing, HVAC, finishes, external areas, common areas, and documentation completeness. Canonical: [[phase-8-post-purchase]].

## B.7 — Off-plan completion & handover checklist

*Alvará de licença de utilização* issued, Câmara final inspection completed, *seguro decenal* bound and certificate delivered, *termo de responsabilidade* signed by builder/engineer/architect, *ficha técnica* and EPC delivered, condominium regulation registered and reserve fund established (if applicable), final price settlement and payment transferred, snagging inspection completed and defect list sent within 30 days, keys/remotes/manuals/warranties inventoried, utilities transferred, insurance bound effective at handover. Canonical: [[phase-8-post-purchase]], [[property-type-new-build-off-plan]].
