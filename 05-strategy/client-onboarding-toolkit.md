# Client onboarding toolkit — questionnaire & BRA template

The two ready-to-use assets behind [[phase-1-onboarding|Phase 1]]'s needs-assessment and BRA-signing subtasks, consolidated from playbook Appendix A. Previously only referenced from the source summary, not transcribed — closed as a standing gap on 2026-07-16. Companion to [[agency-checklists|Appendix B's on-site checklists]] and [[cpcv|Appendix C.3's CPCV clause library]]: this is the front-end paperwork, those are the field-execution and contract layers.

## A.1 — Client onboarding questionnaire

Completed collaboratively in the Phase 1 needs-assessment session: the agent asks, the buyer answers, the agent writes. Feeds directly into the buyer dossier's required sections (see [[phase-1-onboarding]]). Nine intake categories:

- **Personal & contact** — legal name(s) as on passport, nationality, tax residency, existing NIF if any, preferred language and channel.
- **Lifestyle objective classification** — one of six types (primary residence, retirement base, holiday second home, pure investment, hybrid lifestyle+investment, family/education-led); expected annual occupancy; co-buyers/decision-makers; timeline drivers.
- **Financial profile** — all-in budget vs. comfortable working range; source of funds; currency and EUR equivalent; already-liquid vs. needs-transfer; mortgage intention and target LTV; income documentation available.
- **Location preferences** — regions/neighbourhoods, hard proximity requirements (coast, hospital, school), commute tolerance, noise tolerance, orientation.
- **Property specifications** — type, minimum bed/bath/area, outdoor space, parking, lift requirement, condition band, year-built preference, energy-rating tolerance, building size.
- **Use & income intentions** — owner-occupy full/part-time, long-term rental, AL short-term rental, commercial use, family use pattern.
- **Deal-breakers** — explicit disqualifying features (no lift above 2nd floor, ground floor, busy road, leasehold, unlicensed alterations, F/G energy rating, non-renewable AL, etc.).
- **Decision-making & communication** — who else is involved, offer-approval mechanism, co-buyer dispute resolution, preferred cadence and language.
- **Risk appetite** — tolerance for off-plan, renovation scope, off-market purchases, title/licensing complexity, heritage-permit properties.
- **Visa & residency (non-EU only)** — current status, intended pathway (D7/D8/work permit/family reunification/existing golden visa), immigration lawyer engaged, property's relevance to the pathway.

> [!warning] The agent does not provide visa, tax, or immigration advice at any point in this questionnaire — captured here only to route the buyer to the right specialist. See [[phase-1-onboarding]]'s residency-pathway table.

## A.2 — Buyer Representation Agreement (BRA) — outline template

Fifteen-section contract template; requires Portuguese-lawyer review before first use, adapted per engagement. No BRA, no work — this is the mandate [[phase-1-onboarding]] treats as the phase's gating deliverable.

| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| Parties | Buyer(s) name/NIF; Agent/Agency name, AMI number, IMPIC registration |
| Scope of services | Phase-by-phase inclusions (sourcing, viewings, DD coordination, negotiation, transaction management, post-purchase); explicit exclusions (legal, tax, mortgage, immigration advice) |
| Geographic & property-type scope | Regions and property types covered; explicit exclusions |
| Exclusivity | Exclusive/non-exclusive/sole; buyer's obligation to refer directly-identified properties; breach consequences |
| Term | Initial term (example: 6 months); renewal; early-termination triggers |
| Fees | Fee model, trigger event, payment terms, VAT, expense allocation |
| Termination | For-cause and for-convenience terms; fee survival on properties introduced during term (typically 90 days post-termination) |
| Fiduciary duties | Loyalty, confidentiality, disclosure, accounting, obedience, reasonable care; dual-agency consent requirement |
| Buyer obligations | Accurate information, no direct negotiation with listing agents, referral of directly-identified properties |
| Conflict of interest | Disclosure of listing relationships; referral-fee disclosure |
| Confidentiality & GDPR | Lawful basis, data-subject rights, retention schedule, breach notification |
| Dispute resolution | Mediation first; arbitration/courts; Portuguese governing law |
| Notices | Valid notice channels and addresses |
| Entire agreement & amendments | Written-addendum-only amendment clause |
| Counterparts & signatures | Execution mechanics |

### Fee clauses (modular, pick per engagement)

- **Success fee, capped at offer acceptance:** a percentage (playbook example: 1.5%) of final purchase price, calculated on the escritura price but *capped at the price agreed when the seller accepted the buyer's offer* — so a later bid escalation or renegotiated increase never inflates the agent's fee. Invoiced at escritura, payable within a defined window (example: 14 days), plus IVA.
- **Flat fee:** fixed total split across signing / CPCV / escritura tranches; if no property is acquired during the term, only the initial tranche is due.
- **Shared listing commission disclosure:** where the agent also receives a share of the seller-paid listing commission, the source and amount must be disclosed to the buyer in writing within a defined window (example: 5 business days) of receipt — disclosure does not alter the agent's fiduciary duties under the fiduciary-duties section.

See [[phase-1-onboarding]] for the fee-model comparison table (success fee vs. flat vs. retainer+reduced-success vs. value-based vs. shared-commission) and the structural-conflict warnings around percentage-of-price incentives.
