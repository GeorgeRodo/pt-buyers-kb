# Non-inspector hiring — legal, ops, sales, tech, finance

The five capability layers InspectOS needs beyond inspectors themselves, sequenced on a heavy-outsourcing-first philosophy appropriate to a pre-seed/seed proptech startup: don't build payroll bloat, buy expertise on retainer until volume justifies a full-time hire.

## Priority sequencing

**Non-negotiable from Day 1:** external legal counsel (€2,000–€5,000/month retainer — property inspections produce court-admissible reports, and contract templates shouldn't go live unreviewed) and an outsourced Data Protection Officer (€300–€800/month — GDPR Article 38 requires DPO independence, and CNPD fines run up to €20M or 4% of global turnover). **First full-time non-inspector hire (Month 2–4):** an Operations Manager (€35,000–€45,000/year), often combined with compliance duties early on — this person orchestrates scheduling, quality audits, and inspector-network management, and without them the founder becomes the bottleneck once headcount exceeds 8–10 inspectors. **Month 2:** a fractional CTO (€2,000–€4,000/month retainer, 20 hours) for platform architecture and technical strategy — a full-time CTO is judged overkill at this stage. **Once revenue justifies it:** Sales/BD (€28,000–€40,000/year base + 10–20% commission on new B2B deals) and outsourced bookkeeping (€299–€600/month).

## Budget summary

| Function | Year 1 range | First hire timing |
|---|---|---|
| Legal & compliance | €45,600–€99,600 | Immediate (external) |
| Operations | €56,000–€74,000 | Month 3 (first FT hire) |
| Sales & marketing | €62,400–€94,000 | Month 4–5 |
| Technology | €54,000–€72,000 | Month 2 (fractional) |
| Finance & admin | €26,400–€45,600 | Immediate (external) |

Aggressive ramp-up totals ~€192,000 for Year 1; a more conservative sequencing (delaying non-critical hires 2–3 months) brings this to €140,000–€160,000. Headcount grows from ~8 (mostly external/contracted) at Month 1 to ~25–30 (a mix of FT, contractor, and external) at Month 12.

## Integration with inspector recruitment

This track is explicitly interdependent with the inspector-hiring pipeline in [[hiring-90day-roadmap]]: the Operations Manager executes the [[hiring-onboarding-retention|90-day onboarding journey]], external legal counsel reviews every contract template before it's used in [[hiring-quality-gates|vetting]], and the DPO covers GDPR compliance for the inspector and client personal data the platform processes.
