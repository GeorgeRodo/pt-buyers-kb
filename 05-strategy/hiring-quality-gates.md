# Quality gates — the vetting pipeline

Seven gates from first candidate contact to approved-inspector status, 15–45 days depending on candidate type. The non-negotiable constraint underneath every gate: only an OE-registered engineer with active professional liability insurance can sign a court-admissible inspection report — or a candidate can work under the direct supervision of one, with the registered engineer signing.

## The gates

1. **OE verification.** Confirm membership number, specialty, professional title (Engenheiro Nível 1/2, Sénior, or Especialista — all carry full signing authority), and — critically — that the candidate has requested the **professional exercise declaration** from Ageas Portugal. Without it, OE's free group insurance covers only €1,000/incident; with it, €75,000/incident. A candidate who can't produce OE membership, whose registration is suspended, or who lacks the declaration is disqualified or redirected to a trainee track.
2. **Profile-specific check.** Portuguese candidates proceed directly; Brazilian candidates are verified against CREA status and, if OEP admission is still pending, placed in an interim trainee/contractor-support role (they may assist and document but **cannot legally sign reports** until OEP admission completes — this is a hard legal line, not a preference); Angolan candidates go straight to a supervised support role with no independent signing authority, ever, absent the full DGES degree-recognition process.
3. **Background & reference check.** Criminal record, OE ethical standing (no sanctions in the past 5 years), and reference questions targeted at diagnostic skill and report-writing quality rather than generic fit.
4. **Technical proficiency assessment.** Digital tool competency (the InspectOS platform, photo documentation, GPS logging) and Portuguese building-code knowledge (DL 10/2024, RTIEBT, DL 97/2017, DL 101-D/2020, RGEU).
5. **Trial inspection.** A standardized property (80–150m², 15–40 years old, within 30 minutes of the office) seeded with at least 8 pre-planned defects across six categories (structural, humidity, electrical, gas, envelope, urbanism/compliance). Scored on a 100-point rubric across defect-detection rate (30%), severity classification (20%), report quality (25%), regulatory knowledge (15%), and communication (10%). 80+ = full approval; 65–79 = conditional pass with 2–4 supervised inspections; 50–64 = retrain and re-trial in 30 days; below 50 = disqualify (or offer a trainee track if potential is evident).
6. **Insurance & liability framework.** OE's group policy (Ageas Portugal, policy 8410226152, €75,000/incident with the professional exercise declaration) is the foundation; InspectOS layers corporate professional liability, general liability, cyber liability, and D&O coverage on top.
7. **Background/reference and technical proficiency run in parallel** with the trial inspection scheduling to compress the 15–45 day timeline.

## Compliance risk matrix — the three highest-risk failure points

(1) An inspector's OE membership lapsing mid-employment, silently invalidating every report they sign afterward — mitigated by monthly automated status checks. (2) A Brazilian candidate inspecting before OEP admission completes — mitigated by an explicit contract clause and supervisor oversight; the platform itself should refuse to let unregistered profiles digitally sign. (3) The insurance declaration never being obtained, leaving a candidate covered at only €1,000 instead of €75,000 — mitigated by mandatory verification before the first inspection.
