# B2B partnerships

Bypass the consumer; go to the people who handle the money.

## Target 1 — Real estate lawyers (*advogados/solicitadores*)
Lawyers spot registry issues but can't read a wall. Pitch: "We provide the forensic evidence (*Perícia Extrajudicial*) you need to win DL 67/2023 hidden-defect suits. We speak Eurocode, you speak Civil Code." A pre-litigation private expert report establishes scientific facts and forces settlement; if it reaches trial, court-appointed *peritos* (OE/OA) are overworked and their *Relatório Pericial* carries decisive weight — early private expertise shapes the battlefield.

## Target 2 — Premium real estate agencies
Pitch: "We protect your reputation by catching Simplex-era value engineering before the deed." Their referral of inspections is also a channel into agency mandates.

## Reciprocal network (playbook Ch. 11.5)
Lawyers/notaries are simultaneously B2B clients **and** the top off-market sourcing channel ([[sourcing-strategy|layer 3]]) — the same relationship monetizes twice. Maintain the reciprocation discipline: refer back, log every referral, thank in writing.
