# Hiring & inspector operations

Internal company-building content: how InspectOS actually staffs the inspector network the [[platform-home-inspection|inspection platform]] depends on, plus the non-inspector functions (legal, ops, sales, tech, finance) around it. This is operational/HR material, not buyer-journey or platform-technical content — it exists to serve the venture's ability to deliver, not to feed a buyer-facing page directly. Filed under `05-strategy/` because it's business-building content adjacent to [[monetization-strategy]] and [[sops]], not because it maps onto a buyer-journey phase.

The load-bearing legal fact underneath all of this: only OE-registered engineers (or OA-registered architects) can sign court-admissible inspection reports in Portugal. Every hiring, compensation, and quality-gate decision downstream of that constraint exists to protect it.
