# Content strategy — four pillars

Positioning rule: don't scare buyers away; be the only authority capable of protecting them. Every piece is generated from canonical wiki pages — content is a rendering of the knowledge base, never separately-maintained knowledge.

## Pillar 1 — The Scam Exposer (virality / lead gen)
Targets predatory flippers/developers. "3 signs you're buying an Instagram Renovation in Lisbon" · "Why your new-build EPC is probably a lie" · "Gray vs. pink drywall". Format: TikTok/Reels/Shorts showing field tests (cabinet pull, hammer tap, jump test). Source pages: [[instagram-renovation-detection]], [[scie-fire-safety]], [[adene]].

## Pillar 2 — The Legal Timebomb (trust / premium positioning)
Targets 2023–24 legal changes; sits between lawyers (no building science) and builders (no law). "Simplex: why the government no longer inspects your apartment" · "The 30-day rule that will cost you your lawsuit" · "Why signing a clean Auto de Receção is the biggest mistake". Format: long-form YouTube + blog for HNW and B2B audiences. Source pages: [[dl-10-2024-simplex]], [[dl-67-2023]].

## Pillar 3 — The Era-Specific Autopsy (niche authority)
Decades, not "old houses". "1970s apartments are ticking timebombs (concrete cancer)" · "Zombie buildings of 2008" · "Gaiola Pombalina vs. 1980s concrete in an earthquake". Format: case studies with inspection photos and diagrams. Source pages: [[construction-defects-hub]] era backlog, [[lisbon]].

## Pillar 4 — The Tool Masterclass (proof of competence)
Justifies premium pricing over flashlight competitors. Pin moisture meter · borescope · phenolphthalein rainbow. Format: carousels + Shorts, highly visual.

Downstream: pillar content feeds [[lead-magnets]] which feed [[phase-1-onboarding|Phase 1]] intake for both platforms.

Platform-native posting rules (length, cadence, hooks, hashtags) for each pillar's target platform live in [[social-media-playbook]] — kept separate since those numbers change with platform algorithms, not with our own strategy.
