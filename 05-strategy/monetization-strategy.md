# Monetization strategy

One knowledge base, two platforms, one funnel. Concentration: **the buyer's side of the market** — the structurally underserved side.

## Revenue map across the journey

| Journey stage | Agency revenue | Inspection revenue | Shared funnel role |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1–2 | Mandate fees begin | — | Lead magnets capture; content converts |
| P3 | (in fee) | Viewing companion scans | Inspection findings prove agency value |
| P4 | (in fee) | Full forensic inspection + NDT escalation | The report is the shared core artifact |
| P5–6 | Negotiation = fee justification | Report resale as negotiation dossier | Defects → € reductions, visible ROI |
| P7 | (in fee) | Pre-closing re-verification | — |
| P8 | Ancillary services €2–5k/yr; referrals | Snagging (deadline-driven); Perícia Extrajudicial | Retention cadence feeds all platforms |

## Economics anchors (from sources)

- Agency: ~€10k per €700k mandate; €60–100k+ 5-year client value; referrals = 50–70% of mature mandates.
- Inspection: pricing undetermined (**gap**); differentiator is legal-grade output, justifying premium over flashlight competitors.
- B2B: lawyers buy forensic evidence; agencies buy litigation-avoidance → [[b2b-partnerships]].
