# Phase 1 — Pre-engagement & client onboarding

Everything that happens before a single property is shown. The phase most often skipped under pressure to start searching, and the one whose neglect produces the largest downstream cost. Playbook Ch. 2.

## Subtasks

1. **Lead qualification** — motivation, budget reality, timeline, decision-making authority, AML posture.
2. **Needs assessment & goal setting** — hard vs. soft criteria; use case (residence, second home, AL investment, relocation). Run via the nine-category intake questionnaire in [[client-onboarding-toolkit|the onboarding toolkit]].
3. **Market & process education** — the seller-biased structure, transaction sequence, cost stack. This session is where trust is built and where [[content-strategy|content]] pre-sells the engagement.
4. **Buyer Representation Agreement (BRA)** — written mandate, fee model, exclusivity, fiduciary duties. No BRA, no work. Template outline and fee clauses in [[client-onboarding-toolkit]].
5. **Administrative setup** — NIF, Portuguese bank account, fiscal representative (non-EU), visa lawyer where relevant. Kickoff meeting + twice-weekly 15-minute check-ins until all prerequisites land.

## Red flags (decline or slow down)

- Opaque offshore ownership structures → enhanced DD, lawyer referral before BRA.
- Buyer pressured by a third party and unable to decide independently.
- Requests for the agent to act as fiscal representative or receive client funds — structural conflict and AML violation. Refuse.

## Platform touchpoints

- [[platform-buyers-agency|Agency]]: this is the intake funnel; conversion rate here determines everything downstream.
- [[lead-magnets|Lead magnets]] (Pre-CPCV Defense Kit, Simplex Survival Guide) capture emails from buyers in this phase before they know they need representation.
- Tools: CRM (HubSpot/Pipedrive/Notion), DocuSign, Sumsub/Onfido for KYC, DeepL/sworn translation.
