# Phase 8 — Post-purchase & relationship management

Where the economics of the venture actually live. A €700k transaction fee is ~€10k; five-year client value with referrals, repeat transactions, and ancillary services runs €60k–100k+. Retention is the dominant economic driver. Playbook Ch. 9.

## Subtasks

1. **Handover protocol** — snagging inspection (new builds especially: this is where [[dl-67-2023|DL 67/2023]] *reservas* get formally recorded), welcome pack, vendor directory. Printable checklists: [[agency-checklists|Appendix B.5–B.7]] (handover, snagging, off-plan completion).
2. **Utilities, insurance & warranties** — warranty register per system with expiry dates; multibanco/direct-debit setup for non-residents.
3. **Renovation & project management** — architect + contractor coordination as a sponsored service; pre-purchase permit-feasibility assessment prevents budget fantasy.
4. **Property management & rental setup** — six models compared in the playbook (self-managed + caretaker through AL managed); AL licensing constrained by containment zones post-Mais Habitação, 5-year renewal risk.
5. **Retention cadence** — month 1/3/6/9/12 touchpoints, quarterly newsletter, tax-season reminders, explicit referral asks at day 30, month 6, and anniversary. Referrals generate 50–70% of mature-practice mandates.

## Platform touchpoints

- [[platform-home-inspection|Inspection]]: snagging inspections for new builds are a standalone product with a legal deadline attached — urgency sells.
- [[platform-buyers-agency|Agency]]: service expansion menu (portfolio reviews, management oversight, sale-side representation later).
- [[b2b-partnerships|B2B]]: property managers and lawyers met here become referral sources.
