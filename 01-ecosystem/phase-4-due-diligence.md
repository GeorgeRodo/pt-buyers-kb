# Phase 4 — Comprehensive due diligence

The load-bearing phase of the whole venture. Four DD streams run in parallel; the technical stream is where the [[platform-home-inspection|inspection platform]] lives. Playbook Ch. 5; prompt-chain modules 1–10 supply the technical depth.

## The four DD streams

| Stream | What it verifies | Who executes | Key artifacts |
|---|---|---|---|
| Legal | Title chain, encumbrances, registered vs. actual areas, licensing (licença de utilização), [[condominio-law\|condomínio]] health | Lawyer + agent | Certidão predial, caderneta predial, actas, ficha técnica |
| **Technical** | Structure, envelope, MEP, [[acoustic-defects\|acoustics]], [[seismic-risk\|seismic]], [[scie-fire-safety\|fire/SCIE]], moisture, geology | **Inspector / perito** | Inspection report, NDT results, defect register with photos |
| Financial | True total cost of ownership, condomínio reserve fund adequacy, pending special assessments | Agent + tax advisor | TCO model → [[phase-5-financing\|Phase 5]] |
| Regulatory | AL licence validity/containment zones, PDM zoning, heritage classification, EPC honesty (check ADENE) | Agent + lawyer | RNAAL extract, PDM extract, EPC verification |

## The four-phase inspection sequence (from the master checklist, module 10)

1. **Desktop audit** — registry cross-checks (flag área discrepancies >2%), ADENE EPC verification, RCAAP/licensing paper trail. Before leaving the house.

> [!warning] Playbook Appendix C.4 flags the same check at a >5% area-discrepancy threshold, not >2%. See [[cpcv]] for the reconciliation note — unresolved as of 2026-07-14.
2. **Exterior & structural scan** — quantitative red lines: concrete cracks >0.3mm (Eurocode 2), diagonal/X shear cracks, flat roofs with no 1–2% slope, ETICS with no drip edge, insulation thickness vs. EPC claim.
3. **Interior, MEP & biological scan** — the [[instagram-renovation-detection|flip detector]]: hammer sounding, pin moisture meter (>15–20% WME = active damp), aluminum wiring, polybutylene plumbing, gray vs. pink drywall in escape routes, jump test for floating floors.
4. **Triage matrix** — Tier 1 walk away (unlicenseable land, active shear cracking, radon >200 Bq/m³, fraudulent SCIE in AL). Tier 2 negotiate hard under [[dl-67-2023|DL 67/2023]] (EPC fraud, hidden polybutylene €15–25k reduction, dead flat roof). Tier 3 five-year maintenance plan. Explicit threshold for escalating to an independent *Engenheiro Civil* with *Perito* qualification for NDT (thermography, pachometry, core drilling) **before CPCV**.

## Legal context that makes this phase mandatory

- [[dl-10-2024-simplex|DL 10/2024 (Simplex)]] removed municipal inspection of new builds — private technical DD is now the only verification a buyer gets.
- [[dl-67-2023|DL 67/2023]] gives strong hidden-defect rights but with a strict 30-day notification window — undocumented defects are unlitigable defects.
- [[dl-108-2026|DL 108/2026]] (in force 3 August 2026) removes the habitation license as a deed requirement, adding a documentary-verification layer to the desktop audit above: caderneta vs. physical reality, pre-1951 exemption certificate where claimed, strict "reconstruction" (no area/height/volume increase) vs. "extension" classification for any recent works, PEPU submission/fee-payment status, and contractor IMPIC *Alvará* standing. Treat this as a fifth desktop-audit sub-step until the checklist is formally re-numbered.

## Document checklist and property-type variation

Full DD document set (title, licensing, condominium, tax, specialist reports) in [[agency-checklists|Appendix B.3]]. Which documents matter most, and which extra specialist reports are needed, varies sharply by [[property-types-hub|property type]] — a quinta's water-rights documentation and a new build's decenal-warranty certificate are both "DD documents" but nothing else about them overlaps.

## Risk assessment output

Every DD cycle ends in a written risk matrix: issue, severity, probability, mitigation, price impact, walk-away trigger. This document is the raw material for [[phase-6-offer-cpcv|Phase 6]] negotiation and protective CPCV clauses.
