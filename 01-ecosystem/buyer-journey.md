# The buyer journey

The buyer's side of the Portuguese market decomposes into eight sequential phases (Playbook chapters 2–9). Each phase has its own documents, counterparties, timelines, failure modes, and — critically for this venture — its own monetizable pain. This decomposition is the master frame: platforms, content, and domain knowledge all attach here.

| # | Phase | Core output | Typical duration | Platform touchpoint |
|---|-------|-------------|------------------|---------------------|
| 1 | [[phase-1-onboarding\|Pre-engagement & onboarding]] | Signed BRA, NIF, bank account, client dossier | 2–4 weeks | Agency intake; lead magnets capture here |
| 2 | [[phase-2-sourcing\|Research, strategy & sourcing]] | 8–20 qualified candidates, 15–30% off-market | 4–6 weeks | Agency core work; market-intel content |
| 3 | [[phase-3-viewings\|Evaluation, viewings & shortlisting]] | Shortlist of 2–4 properties | 2–6 weeks | First inspection touchpoint (pre-offer walk-through) |
| 4 | [[phase-4-due-diligence\|Comprehensive due diligence]] | Legal + technical + financial + regulatory clearance | 2–8 weeks | **Inspection platform's home phase** |
| 5 | [[phase-5-financing\|Financial planning & structuring]] | TCO model, mortgage, tax structure | parallel to 4–6 | Referral revenue (mortgage brokers, tax advisors) |
| 6 | [[phase-6-offer-cpcv\|Negotiation, offer & CPCV]] | Signed CPCV with protective clauses | 1–4 weeks | Inspection findings become negotiation leverage |
| 7 | [[phase-7-closing\|Transaction execution & closing]] | Escritura (deed), keys | 4–12 weeks post-CPCV | Pre-closing re-verification inspection |
| 8 | [[phase-8-post-purchase\|Post-purchase & relationship]] | Snagging, handover, retention cadence | ongoing, years | Snagging inspections; recurring revenue; referrals |

## Why this decomposition matters

Three structural facts make each phase a defensible business rather than a commodity service:

1. **Nobody else sits on the buyer's side.** The *mediador imobiliário* is paid by the seller. Every phase is currently navigated by buyers either alone or advised by their adversary's agent. See [[platform-buyers-agency|the agency thesis]].
2. **The state exited technical verification.** [[dl-10-2024-simplex|Simplex]] means new builds are no longer inspected by the Câmara. Phase 4 technical DD moved from public good to private necessity. See [[platform-home-inspection|the inspection thesis]].
3. **Liability clocks punish the uninformed.** [[dl-67-2023|DL 67/2023]] gives buyers real weapons against hidden defects, but only if defects are documented and notified within strict windows. Phases 4, 6 and 8 are where that documentation is created or lost.

## Cross-phase threads

- The **comparable set** built in Phase 2 becomes the offer justification in Phase 6 ([[comparables]]).
- **Defects** found in Phases 3–4 become *reservas* on the *Auto de Receção* and price leverage in Phase 6, then litigation evidence in Phase 8 ([[construction-defects-hub]]).
- **Property type** changes the content of every phase without changing the sequence — Playbook Ch. 10 profiles seven types (historical, off-plan, land, luxury, resale, rural, high-yield).
- **Region** changes the risk profile of Phases 3–4 dramatically ([[regions-hub]]).
