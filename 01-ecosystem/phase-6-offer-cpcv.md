# Phase 6 — Negotiation, offer & CPCV

Where all upstream work converts into money saved and risk contractually neutralized. Playbook Ch. 7.

## Subtasks

1. **Offer strategy** — anchored in the [[comparables|comparable set]] from Phase 2 and the defect register from Phase 4. Every euro of discount is justified in writing; asking prices 10%+ above the comparable ceiling require the seller to justify, not the buyer.
2. **CPCV drafting & review** — the promissory contract is the real battlefield; the deed merely executes it. Protective clauses: financing contingency, DD contingency, licensing condition precedent, penalty symmetry (sinal doubling), defect remediation schedules, and — as of [[dl-108-2026|DL 108/2026]] (in force 3 August 2026) — explicit urban-title disclosure mirroring the notary's mandatory deed declaration. Detail: [[cpcv]].
3. **Deposit protection, contingencies & timelines** — sinal sizing, escrow discipline, completion timeline with defined extension mechanics.

## Defects as negotiation currency

Tier 2 findings from the [[phase-4-due-diligence|Phase 4 triage matrix]] convert directly:

- Hidden polybutylene plumbing → €15k–25k price reduction demand.
- EPC fraud (measured insulation < declared) → slash price; post-build insulation retrofit is disproportionately expensive.
- Failed flat roof (no slope, alligatored membrane) → full replacement cost off the price.
- Everything accepted-but-unfixed goes in as *reservas* on the *Auto de Receção Provisória*, arming the [[dl-67-2023|DL 67/2023]] 30-day notification mechanism.

## Platform touchpoints

- [[platform-buyers-agency|Agency]]: negotiation outcome is the most legible proof of fee value.
- [[platform-home-inspection|Inspection]]: the inspection report is literally the negotiation dossier — bundle pricing opportunity.
