# Phase 3 — Evaluation, viewings & shortlisting

Converts the candidate pipeline into a shortlist of 2–4 properties for due diligence. Playbook Ch. 4.

## Subtasks

1. **Viewing preparation** — pre-pull caderneta predial, certidão predial, energy certificate; build the viewing checklist per property type — see [[property-types-hub]] for the six type profiles and [[agency-checklists|Appendix B]] for the printable checklist.
2. **Viewing execution** — structured walk-through with the buyer-side eye: orientation, noise, [[instagram-renovation-detection|flip-detection quick tests]] (hammer tap, cabinet pull, under-sink check), visual structural red flags.
3. **Debrief & shortlisting** — written debrief within 24h; scoring against hard/soft criteria; kill fast.
4. **Professional third-party assessments** — the trigger point where the [[platform-home-inspection|inspection platform]] enters: a pre-offer technical walk-through on shortlisted properties, before money or exclusivity is committed.

## Why this phase feeds the inspection platform

The cheapest defect is one found before the offer. Quick-scan findings here either kill a candidate (saving the buyer the full DD cost) or scope the full Phase 4 inspection. This makes the light-touch "viewing companion scan" a natural entry-level inspection product — see [[inspection-in-the-buyer-journey]].

## Red flags at viewing

- Listing agent pushes for an immediate offer during the first viewing.
- Freshly painted single walls or new plaster in isolated patches (moisture concealment).
- Furniture placed hard against exterior walls (mold concealment) — check behind.
- Hollow-sounding new plasterboard over old walls; pristine kitchen over unrenovated carcass under the sink.
