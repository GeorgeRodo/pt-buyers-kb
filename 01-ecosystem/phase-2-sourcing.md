# Phase 2 — Research, strategy & property sourcing

Turns the buyer's requirements into a structured market-intelligence operation. Done well: 8–20 qualified candidates in 4–6 weeks, 15–30% of eventually-shown properties sourced off-market. Playbook Ch. 3.

## Subtasks

1. **Deep market analysis** — continuously updated, not one-time. Portals show asking prices only; 15–35% of transactions happen off-market or developer-direct, so portal-only analysis is structurally biased. Full component table in [[market-intelligence]].
2. **Five-layer sourcing stack** — portals (60–75% of market), agent network (10–20%), off-market direct-to-owner (10–20%), developer-direct (10–15%), bank/institutional distressed (1–5%). Detail in [[sourcing-strategy]].
3. **Custom search brief** — written filter translating the dossier into daily scanning criteria; tuned per [[property-types-hub|property type]] — a Príncipe Real apartment brief and an Alentejo quinta brief share no template.
4. **Cadence & communication** — daily portal scan logged in CRM, weekly agent-network outbound, bi-weekly written buyer report, monthly market one-pager, 24h post-viewing debriefs.

## Red flags

- Listing agent refuses caderneta predial or energy certificate pre-viewing.
- Relisted higher within 30 days of a reduction (manufactured demand).
- Asking 25%+ above comparables with no articulated justification.
- "Off-market" opportunity that three other agents also have.
- 48-hour "early-bird" reservation pressure from developers.
- Listed >180 days with no reduction.
