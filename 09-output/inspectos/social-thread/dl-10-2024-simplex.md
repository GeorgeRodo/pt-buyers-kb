# Thread — "Nobody is inspecting your new-build apartment. Nobody."

**1/**
Since DL 10/2024 (Simplex), the Câmara Municipal no longer performs technical inspection of new-build construction in Portugal. Licensing moved to self-certification by the developer's own technicians — *termos de responsabilidade*. The state signed off on trusting the builder to check the builder's own work.

**2/**
This isn't a bureaucratic footnote. It's the mechanism behind the material-substitution patterns we document constantly: SCIE fire-rated cores swapped for standard drywall, acoustic pre-certification issued for a spec that never gets built.

**3/**
Câmaras don't deploy specialized engineers to physically verify construction anymore — so "value engineering" on 2024+ builds goes uncaught unless the buyer independently commissions a private *perito* (technical expert) to check.

**4/**
Practical result: for anything built under this regime, private inspection isn't a comfort purchase. It's the only verification layer that exists between "what the plans say" and "what got built."

**5/**
DL 10/2024 is phase one of a two-phase deregulation. [[dl-108-2026|DL 108/2026]] (in force 3 August 2026) goes further — removing state *documentary* verification at the deed stage too, so even the paper trail buyers used to lean on thins out.

**6/**
If you're buying new-build in Portugal post-2024, the question isn't "did the state check this." It didn't. The question is whether anyone independent did — and that's the gap forensic inspection exists to close.
