# Thread — "Starting August 3, the paperwork check at your deed disappears"

**1/**
Big change coming to Portugal's property market: starting 3 August 2026, you can execute a deed without the property having a habitation license. The notary just writes down whether one exists, is claimed, or is admittedly missing. The state stopped checking.

**2/**
Here's why that matters to you specifically: silence from the municipality now counts as approval. But the government can still come back — with no time limit — and order changes demolished. "Approved" and "safe forever" are no longer the same thing.

**3/**
And it's not just the seller's problem after closing. Liability for non-compliant construction is now joint across promoter, owner, designer, and contractor. When a contractor's company folds (common in Portugal), the fine — up to €450,000 — lands on whoever's still standing. That could eventually be you.

**4/**
This is creating a real three-tier market: fully documented properties selling at a 5–10% premium, tacitly-approved ones at a 3–8% discount, and undocumented ones at 20–40% off, cash-only. Same neighborhood, same square footage, very different risk — and the listing price won't tell you which tier you're looking at.

**5/**
Banks already know this. Even though the deed no longer requires the license, lenders are still asking for it before they'll finance — which means the paperwork gap that just opened for sellers hasn't closed for buyers. You still need someone checking it.

**6/**
The buyers who get hurt by this law aren't the ones buying documented properties at a fair premium. They're the ones who don't know a discount exists because the building isn't fully approved — until the bill for someone else's non-compliance arrives at their door.
