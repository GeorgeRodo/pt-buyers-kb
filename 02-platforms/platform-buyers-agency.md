# Platform 1 — Buyer's agency

Licensed buyer-only representation under a written Buyer Representation Agreement: no seller commission, full fiduciary duties (loyalty, confidentiality, disclosure, accounting, obedience, reasonable care) owed to the buyer alone.

## Thesis

The Portuguese intermediation regime (DL 118/2013) makes the *mediador imobiliário* the seller's commercial counterparty. A buyer without dedicated representation negotiates against the person paid to maximize the price. Post-2023 legislation (Mais Habitação, golden-visa closure, AML tightening) raised transaction complexity while leaving this asymmetry intact — widening the gap the agency fills.

## Operating model

- **Manual:** the [[src-buyers-agent-playbook-2026|2026 playbook]] end to end — the 8 phases of [[buyer-journey|the journey]] are the service delivery pipeline.
- **Economics:** ~1.5% or fixed fee per mandate (€10k on €700k); 5-year client value €60k–100k+ via referrals (50–70% of mature-practice mandates), repeat transactions, ancillary services. Retention cadence in [[phase-8-post-purchase|Phase 8]].
- **Differentiators:** off-market sourcing (15–30% of shown stock), written comparable-justified offers, protective [[cpcv|CPCV]] clause library, TCO modeling, multi-party closing coordination.
- **Property-type specialization:** six type playbooks — [[property-types-hub|historical, off-plan, land, luxury, resale, rural]] — same phases, radically different content per type.

## Fee-setting discipline (playbook §11.8)

Supplements the Ch. 2.4 fee-model options already on record: fee must be commensurate with work — a 1.5% success fee is barely economic on a €300k deal (€4,500) but generous on a €2m deal (€30,000), so low-value transactions should default to flat fee/retainer and high-value ones to flat fee or a capped success fee. Cap the success fee at offer-acceptance to remove any incentive against aggressive price negotiation, and always disclose the fee source (buyer-paid vs. shared listing commission) in writing. Collection: invoice on the BRA-defined trigger (offer acceptance / CPCV / deed); success fees invoice at deed, payable 7–14 days; retainers invoice monthly in advance, payable within 7 days; flat fees invoice in milestone tranches.

## Compliance perimeter

AMI licensing via IMPIC; GDPR; AML/KYC obligations (identity + source of funds); referral-fee disclosure. Never hold client funds; never act as fiscal representative.
