# IMT, IMI & AIMI — Portuguese property taxation

Three distinct taxes a buyer encounters at different points in the journey, consistently cited together across [[phase-5-financing|Phase 5]] TCO modeling, [[phase-7-closing|Phase 7]] pre-closing checklists, and every [[property-types-hub|property-type profile]]. Filed as one page because no source ever discusses one without the others — splitting them would fragment a single buyer-facing tax conversation.

> [!warning] Yearly re-verification required
> IMT brackets, IMI rates, and AIMI thresholds change with each State Budget (*Lei do Orçamento do Estado*). Never quote a figure from this page without checking the current year — see the same standing warning on [[phase-5-financing]].

## IMT — *Imposto Municipal sobre Transmissões* (transfer tax)

One-time tax paid at the deed, 1–8% of the purchase price depending on property value and use (progressive brackets, primary vs. secondary residence, urban vs. rústico). Payable alongside *Imposto do Selo* (stamp duty, flat 0.8% of price) — together these are the two taxes due before the notary will proceed. New builds carry VAT (23%) instead of/alongside IMT depending on structure — see [[property-type-new-build-off-plan]].

## IMI — *Imposto Municipal sobre Imóveis* (annual municipal tax)

Recurring annual tax, 0.3–0.45% of the *Valor Patrimonial Tributário* (VPT, the fiscal tax value, distinct from market price) for urban property, ~0.8% for rústico (rural) land — see the lower-IMI note on [[property-type-rural-quinta]]. Set by each municipality within those bands, so it varies year to year and by location. Confirmed cleared to the deed date is a standing pre-closing checklist item (seller's IMI arrears attach as an AT lien) — see [[phase-7-closing]] and [[agency-checklists]]. Registration updates to the new owner post-deed within 1–30 days.

Older housing stock can carry a *lower* IMI than new builds despite similar market value, because VPT is based on older *matriz* assessments — see the historical-property and new-build comparisons on [[property-type-historical]] and [[property-type-new-build-off-plan]].

## AIMI — *Adicional ao IMI* (wealth surcharge)

Surcharge on high-value residential property owned by **individuals** (not companies — companies instead pay IMI at a flat 1.5% on full asset value), applying above roughly €600k of secondary-home value at 0.7–1.5%. Relevant to HNW and luxury-property buyers regardless of era or condition — a historical-centre apartment with a low base IMI can still trigger AIMI if the aggregate value clears the threshold.

## Where this feeds the buyer journey

TCO modeling in [[phase-5-financing]] treats IMT + stamp duty as the one-time floor above the purchase price, and IMI (+ AIMI where applicable) as the recurring cost that differentiates a genuinely comparable TCO across property types — a differentiating deliverable, not a formality. [[phase-7-closing]]'s pre-closing verification step confirms seller IMI is cleared before funds move. Tax residency status (see [[client-onboarding-toolkit]] intake) drives IRS treatment of related income but is out of scope for direct advice — buyers get referred to a tax advisor.
