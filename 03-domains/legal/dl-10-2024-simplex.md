# DL 10/2024 — Simplex

The urbanistic simplification package. Its operative consequence for this venture: **the state no longer performs technical inspection of new builds** — licensing shifted toward self-certification by the developer's own technicians (*termos de responsabilidade*).

## Why it creates the inspection market

- The Câmara Municipal lacks (and no longer deploys) specialized engineers to physically verify construction — enabling the self-certification loopholes documented across the defect modules (e.g., SCIE material substitution, acoustic pre-cert bait-and-switch).
- "Value engineering" on 2024+ builds is now caught by nobody unless the buyer hires a private *perito*. Private inspection moved from optional comfort to the only verification layer that exists.
