# Instagram renovation detection

> **Client sees:** stunning photos, new kitchen, fresh paint, "fully renovated". | **Actual issue:** cosmetic skin over degraded structure, damp masonry, and MEP timebombs. | **Typical era:** flips 2015+, especially of 1950s–80s stock. | **Tool needed:** steel hammer, pin moisture meter, screwdriver, eyes. | **Legal weapon:** [[dl-67-2023|DL 67/2023]] — but only if found and documented pre-purchase.

## The sequential scan (module 3 synthesis; runs at [[phase-3-viewings|P3]] quick-form, [[phase-4-due-diligence|P4]] full-form)

1. **Hammer sounding.** Tap new plaster and baseboards — hollow = plasterboard hiding degraded *reboco* or *tabique*.
2. **Under-sink truth.** Kitchen cabinets/sinks sit over the unrenovated carcass; original damp masonry there = purely cosmetic renovation.
3. **Moisture mapping.** Pin meter at exterior-wall bases and behind heavy furniture; **>15–20% wood-moisture-equivalent = active rising damp or condensation**.
4. **Corner check.** Upper corners of exterior walls, behind curtains/wardrobes: black *bolor* = thermal-bridge failure.
5. **Electrical panel.** Silver conductors stamped "Al"/"1A/2A" = aluminum wiring, major fire hazard.
6. **Plumbing.** Gray/orange pipe stamped "PB"/"Polibutileno" = imminent flood risk; hidden-in-wall polybutylene = €15–25k negotiation item ([[phase-6-offer-cpcv|P6]]).
7. **Acoustic jump test** and **SCIE core/tag checks** → [[acoustic-defects]], [[scie-fire-safety]].
