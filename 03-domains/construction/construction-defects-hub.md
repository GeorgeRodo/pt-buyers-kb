# Construction defects hub

The technical engine of the [[platform-home-inspection|inspection platform]] and the substance behind [[phase-4-due-diligence|Phase 4]]. Source: the 12-module / 48-prompt forensic chain (see [[src-construction-defects-prompt-chain|source summary]] for module map).

## Filing rules

1. **Symptom-first, always.** Every defect page opens with: client sees → actual issue → typical era → tool needed → legal weapon. A page organized by chemistry is a page nobody can use in the field.
2. **Numbers are sacred.** 0.3mm crack thresholds, Rw > 53 dB, 15–20% WME, 200 Bq/m³ — never rounded or paraphrased away.
3. **Every page links** to: phase(s), platform(s), legal weapon, affected regions.
4. **Era tagging.** Portuguese pathology is decade-specific (1970s concrete cancer, 2008 zombie buildings, 2015+ Instagram renos, 2024+ Simplex builds). Talk decades, not "old houses".
