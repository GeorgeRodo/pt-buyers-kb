# Acoustic defects & the RRAA illusion

> **Client sees:** hears neighbours talking through the wall; hears every upstairs footstep. | **Actual issue:** single-leaf party walls, rigidly-bonded floors, flanking transmission — RRAA non-compliance. | **Typical era:** all eras; fraud pattern acute in post-2000 builds and AL conversions. | **Tool needed:** pink-noise speaker + decibel meter (ISO 16283 field method); jump test. | **Legal weapon:** [[dl-67-2023|DL 67/2023]] *vícios ocultos* claim backed by field measurement.

## The four failure mechanisms (module 7.1)

1. **Single-leaf party wall (*parede simples*).** The standard 15–20cm plastered ceramic-block *parede meeira* routinely fails the RRAA (DL 129/2002) airborne requirement of **Rw > 53 dB** — insufficient mass, resonant-frequency failure.
2. **Impact noise.** Floating floors (*piso flutuante*) rigidly bonded to the slab, or missing resilient underlay (*manta resiliente*), transmit impact energy straight through the *laje*, breaching **L'n,w < 62 dB**.
3. **Flanking transmission.** Sound travels laterally through continuous slabs and lightweight façades, bypassing the party wall entirely — insulating the party wall alone fixes nothing if flanking paths aren't decoupled.
4. **The pre-certification scam.** Developers obtain a *Pré-Certificação Acústica* from theoretical software models, then substitute specified acoustic mats with cheap foam on site. Paper compliance, illegal building. Enabled by the self-certification environment of [[dl-10-2024-simplex|Simplex]].

## Field verification

- Jump test: jump on the floor; if the neighbour below hears it clearly, the mandatory floating floor was skipped ([[phase-4-due-diligence|P4 interior scan]]).
- ISO 16283 field test (pink noise + dB meter across units) produces court-grade evidence of RRAA failure.
- Demand the **final** *Certificado de Classificação Acústica*, not the pre-cert, before CPCV.
