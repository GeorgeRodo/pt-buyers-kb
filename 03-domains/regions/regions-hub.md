# Regions hub

Portugal is regional to a degree that surprises foreign buyers: pricing, supply elasticity, demand source, regulation, and — the inspection platform's edge — **geology and pathology** all vary by region. Each regional dossier combines market character + construction risk + regulatory quirks, per Paul's "tag by geography" rule.

## Dossiers

- [[lisbon|Lisbon]] — liquefaction alluvium, Pombalino vs. Placa stock, AL containment zones.
- [[porto|Porto]] — granite + acid rain, heavy rising damp, historic-centre containment.
- [[algarve|Algarve]] — expansive clays, coastal salinity, polybutylene era, AL economics.
