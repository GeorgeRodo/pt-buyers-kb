# Algarve

## Geology & pathology

- **Expansive clays (*argilas expansivas*).** Montmorillonite-bearing marl/clay in the interior swells when wet, shrinks when dry; the cycle heaves shallow foundations (*fundações diretas*) and produces continuous **step-cracking** that rebar cannot resist. Correct answer is piles (*estacas*) to stable strata — routinely skipped by cheap developers.
- **Coastal salinity attack.** Sulfates form expansive ettringite in concrete; chlorides accelerate rebar corrosion — rust stains (*manchas de oxidação*) on ground-floor concrete are the field tell.
- **High water tables** (Lagos, Quarteira): hydrostatic uplift cracks and floats un-anchored basement slabs.
- **Polybutylene era plumbing** concentrated in Algarve stock — the €15–25k [[phase-6-offer-cpcv|negotiation item]].

## Market & regulation

- Demand dominated by foreign lifestyle + AL investors; Quinta do Lago / Vale do Lobo run luxury-segment dynamics (Ch. 10.4); AL containment applies on parts of the coast.
- Foreign-buyer concentration makes the Algarve the natural first market for English-language [[lead-magnets|lead magnets]] and the [[platform-home-inspection|inspection platform]].

## Inspection emphasis

Step-crack pattern scan (clay heave signature) · rust-stain scan at grade · foundation-type verification on anything interior · under-sink PB check by default.
