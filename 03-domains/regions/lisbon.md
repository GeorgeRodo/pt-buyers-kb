# Lisbon

## Geology & pathology

- **Liquefaction risk** in Tagus-alluvium zones (Baixa Pombalina, Chelas, parts of Alcântara): saturated loose sands lose shear strength in an earthquake, buildings sink or tilt. LNEC seismic microzonation maps are the reference — check the zone before pricing structural risk.
- **Stock eras:** *Gaiola Pombalina* timber-cage buildings (seismically clever, 300 years old, condition-dependent) vs. *Placa* era vs. 1960s–80s concrete frames with the [[seismic-risk|soft-story problem]] — decade determines pathology.

## Market & regulation

- Prime central segments internationally driven; AL licensing **suspended in containment zones** (Baixa, Chiado, Alfama, Bairro Alto, Príncipe Real…) — kills the AL investment case for unlicensed stock; existing licences face 5-year renewal risk.
- Historic-centre stock triggers the heavy-DD property-type profile (heritage classification, PDM constraints, renovation permit feasibility).

## Inspection emphasis

Microzone check in the desktop audit · era identification before the visit · [[seismic-risk|soft-story scan]] on 60s–80s frames · [[instagram-renovation-detection|flip forensics]] on renovated Alfama/Bairro stock.
