# Porto

## Geology & pathology

- **Granite building stock + acid rain**: surface degradation of stone façades; check pointing and stone face condition.
- **Heavy rising damp**: the north's rainfall regime makes moisture the default pathology — pin-meter mapping at wall bases is non-negotiable in every Porto inspection ([[instagram-renovation-detection|flip scan step 3]]).
- Altitude/interior micro-climates add freeze-thaw spalling of porous renders (*ciclos gelo-degelo*, 9% expansion on freezing).

## Market & regulation

- AL containment zones in the historic centre (Ribeira, Aliados, Cedofeita) — same licensing analysis as [[lisbon|Lisbon]]; Ribeira sits in a UNESCO buffer zone with strict heritage control on top of the AL restriction.
- Renovated historic-centre flips are the dominant local [[instagram-renovation-detection|Instagram-reno]] risk pool.
- **Porto (city)**: demand mix of tourism, remote work, and domestic relocation; historic centre is supply-constrained with peripheral new build taking the overflow; price direction is modest growth, centre resilient. Domestic and foreign demand are roughly balanced here — unlike Lisbon's foreign-dominated centre. The *gaioleiro* timber-floor structural type ([[property-type-historical]]) is unique to Porto and requires surveyors with specific experience — a generalist Lisbon-trained surveyor's checklist doesn't transfer.
- **Porto metropolitan (Matosinhos, Vila Nova de Gaia, Maia)**: family relocation and coastal-lifestyle driven; active new-build supply; stable pricing, generally lower than Lisbon-metro equivalents. Matosinhos and Gaia waterfront command a coastal premium within the metro area.
