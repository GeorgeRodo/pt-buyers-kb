# Property type — new build & off-plan

Concentrated in Lisbon's Parque das Nações, Loures and Odivelas expansion zones, Porto's Antas/Campanhã/Matosinhos, the Algarve's modern resort developments, and Silver Coast coastal developments. Buyers here want turnkey condition, energy efficiency, and (for off-plan) payment-plan flexibility. The DD profile inverts the resale logic: instead of inspecting an existing building, the buyer is assessing a **developer's reliability**, construction specifications, the legal guarantee framework, and the project's completion trajectory. This is also the segment most exposed by [[dl-10-2024-simplex|DL 10/2024]] — with municipal inspection of new builds gone, private technical DD is the only verification a buyer gets.

## Distinctive due diligence

- **Developer reliability** — track record, financial health, corporate filings, buyer references, bankruptcy/litigation history.
- **Construction specifications** — materials, structural system, MEP, insulation, finishes; what's included vs. optional upgrade pricing.
- **Completion guarantee** — a bank guarantee (*garantia bancária*) or insurance policy covering deposit and progress payments against developer default is **mandatory** under Decree-Law 156/2005 for residential off-plan.
- **Payment plan** — structure and alignment with construction progress; conditions if milestones slip.
- **Penalty clauses** — late-delivery penalty, typically 0.05–0.2%/day, capped.
- **Licences** — *alvará de licença de construção* issued; Câmara project approval.
- **Decenal warranty & insurance** — *seguro decenal* (10-year structural warranty insurance), mandatory under DL 113/2013; *termo de responsabilidade*; *ficha técnica de habitação*.
- **VAT treatment** — first transmission within 5 years is subject to 23% VAT, typically embedded in price; verify invoicing and IMT implications.
- **Snagging rights** — buyer's right to inspect within 30 days of handover; developer must remediate within 30 days; hold the final payment until snagging is complete.

## Common red flags

- Developer with no completed projects or a recently-formed company — high default risk.
- No DL 156/2005 bank guarantee or insurance for progress payments — non-compliant; refuse to proceed.
- Vague specifications ("high-quality finishes") without brand/material specifics.
- Reservation contract demanding a non-refundable deposit >10% before CPCV.
- Aggressive "early-bird" pricing with a 48-hour deadline — manufactured urgency, nearly always negotiable.
- "Estimated" handover date with no liquidated damages for delay.
- Sales rep unable to produce the *alvará de licença de construção* — the licence may not yet be issued.

## Recommended specialists

Lawyer with documented off-plan contract and DL 156/2005 experience; independent snagging surveyor (not the developer's representative); for high-value off-plan, an independent quantity surveyor to verify construction progress against payment milestones.

## Cost-of-ownership nuances

VAT (23%) included in price, but IMT/IS still payable on it; lower immediate renovation and maintenance cost for the first 5–7 years; higher VPT than older stock can mean higher IMI per m² despite the lower rate; condominium fees run higher for modern amenities (pool, gym, concierge, 24h security).

## Negotiation tactics

Price discount is usually harder to win than concessions — push parking, storage, finishings upgrades, appliance packages instead. Negotiate the penalty clause upward (developer defaults are often minimal by design): demand 0.1%/day minimum, capped at 5–10% of price. For slow-selling developments, negotiate deposit reduction, longer payment plans, or handover-condition clauses.

## Region-specific notes

Lisbon: Parque das Nações mature, Loures/Odivelas active pipeline. Porto: Antas/Campanhã redevelopment, Matosinhos coastal premium. Algarve: branded residences common, verify developer track record. Silver Coast: emerging developments at lower entry price — verify developer solvency carefully.
