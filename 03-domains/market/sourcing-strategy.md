# Five-layer sourcing stack

Multi-channel by design; portal-only sourcing means competing on the listing agent's terms. Mature mix: 40–60% portals, 20–30% agent-network referrals, 10–20% off-market, balance developer/institutional.

1. **Portals** (60–75% of stock) — saved searches, price-reduction alerts, daily scan logged.
2. **Agent network** (10–20%) — pre-launch stock via relationships, WhatsApp groups, reciprocation discipline.
3. **Off-market direct-to-owner** (10–20%) — micro-market letter drops (1–3 serious responses per committed street), lawyer/notary probate referrals, selective door-knocking. Discipline: off-market ≠ discount; sellers trade discretion for price. Document introductions in writing to protect commission position.
4. **Developer-direct** (10–15%) — pre-launch access, off-plan payment-plan negotiation.
5. **Bank & institutional** (1–5%) — repossessions; speed of execution decisive.
